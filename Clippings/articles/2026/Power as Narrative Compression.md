## A note on authority and scale

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*PKBg2ETPpv50BKBFMK7JRg.png)

What does power look like when it operates as a measurable capacity rather than a posture or claim? Power is the ability to move a decision forward while alternative paths remain uncarried and the surrounding system absorbs the resulting load. Action proceeds on accumulated momentum, and unchosen options persist as background pressure rather than active constraint. As justification loses immediacy, explanation shifts its timing and direction. Language turns toward reconstruction, arranging causes around outcomes that already occupy space and continue to shape what follows.

Hindsight is the preferred raw material. After outcomes settle, events line up cleanly. Noise drops out. Accidents fade. Timing looks intentional. The path taken appears singular. Hindsight produces a smooth surface that feels explanatory even when it’s merely descriptive.

Counterfactuals disrupt that surface. They reintroduce branching. They force attention to roads not taken, luck, near misses, structural tailwinds, and contingent timing. Counterfactual thinking keeps causal claims honest. It also weakens authority, because authority depends on inevitability.

That relationship can be written simply:

> power = hindsight ÷ counterfactuals

This is not metaphor. It’s a mechanical description of how narrative authority behaves under pressure.

As counterfactuals approach zero, power increases rapidly. When alternative explanations are excluded, hindsight expands to fill the gap. The resulting story feels complete. It feels earned. It feels instructive. It also carries less information than it appears to.

People who accumulate authority often retell their own histories this way. Success becomes proof of insight. Failure disappears into irrelevance. Environmental conditions shrink into background texture. The listener receives a clean arc with a protagonist at its center. That arc stabilizes the present position of the speaker. It removes the need to consider what else might have produced the same outcome.

This pattern *scales.*

In small systems, counterfactuals are easy to surface. One person can imagine an alternative decision. A team can replay a missed turn. Feedback arrives quickly. Power remains fragile.

Large systems behave differently. Scale stretches feedback loops. Outcomes lag decisions by years. Attribution becomes murky. Participants lose access to ground truth. Under those conditions, hindsight becomes efficient. It compresses complexity into something manageable. Power rewards that compression.

Economics at scale runs on this dynamic.

Market winners often narrate dominance as foresight. Timing, access to capital, regulatory shelter, network effects, and demand shocks fade into footnotes. What remains is a story of insight, discipline, and vision. That story justifies present concentration. It reassures incumbents. It discourages structural inquiry.

Counterfactual analysis threatens this equilibrium. Asking whether a firm would exist under different interest rates, different trade regimes, different platform rules, or different accident sequences undermines the inevitability narrative. That questioning feels destabilizing. Power responds by minimizing it.

At scale, institutions formalize hindsight. Metrics get selected after outcomes emerge. Models get fit to historical data that already encodes the result. Risk frameworks treat realized paths as representative. Tail events disappear into variance. Learning turns into narrative maintenance.

This does not require malice. It’s a selection effect. Systems reward actors who tell convincing stories about why they sit where they sit. Those stories rely on hindsight. They downweight branching histories. Over time, counterfactual capacity erodes.

Economic concentration accelerates the process. When few entities control capital flows, employment, infrastructure, or platforms, alternative futures become harder to test. Entry barriers rise. Experiments shrink. The observed world collapses toward what already exists. Hindsight strengthens because fewer competing outcomes survive long enough to be remembered.

This helps explain why large-scale economic debates often feel shallow. Participants argue over moral framing while skipping structural contingency. Policy discussions reference past success as proof of correctness. Failure gets attributed to execution errors rather than underlying conditions. Power speaks fluently about history while remaining vague about possibility.

Reintroducing counterfactuals restores signal. It forces acknowledgment of luck, timing, and external scaffolding. It complicates authority. It also improves decision quality. Engineering disciplines understand this intuitively. Postmortems focus on alternative failure modes. Safety depends on imagining what almost happened.

Economics struggles with that mindset at scale because power resists it. Admitting counterfactuals dilutes narrative control. It invites redistribution of credit. It weakens claims of inevitability. That resistance shows up as confidence, certainty, and overfitting to the past.

The equation holds because division matters. Hindsight divided by a large counterfactual set yields modest power. Hindsight divided by a tiny counterfactual set yields dominance. Remove alternatives and stories harden into doctrine.

This framing avoids moral language. It does not accuse individuals of stupidity. It describes incentives. Powerholders do not need to entertain branching worlds. Others do. That asymmetry shapes discourse, policy, and markets.

Understanding this helps clarify why economic systems drift toward technocratic certainty while growing more fragile. Hindsight remains abundant. Counterfactual capacity declines. Power consolidates. When shocks arrive, surprise follows.

Restoring balance requires institutionalized counterfactuals. Scenario analysis with teeth. Structural humility. Mechanisms that preserve alternative futures long enough to matter. Without that, hindsight continues to inflate authority until reality intervenes.

The equation is simple because the mechanism is simple. Power feeds on compressed history. Counterfactuals dilute it. Scale decides which side wins.
