# Anthropic Files for IPO, Valuation Nears $1 Trillion

**Date**: 2026-06-02
**Source cluster**: BBC, CNBC, NYT, Axios, CoinDesk, Business Insider, TechCrunch, MarketWise

## What Happened

Anthropic, the AI company behind Claude, has:
- **Confidentially filed an IPO prospectus with the SEC** (CNBC, 2026-06-01 16:04 GMT) — "prepping Wall Street for landmark AI deal"
- **Plans to sell shares in the US later this year** (BBC, 2026-06-02 07:00 GMT) — "valuation nears $1tn"
- **Already surging on secondary markets** — Business Insider (2026-04-22) reports Anthropic has surpassed OpenAI on secondary-market trading at a trillion-dollar implied valuation.

## Why It Matters

1. **Second trillion-dollar AI listing, post-SpaceX**: Together with SpaceX's planned June 12 IPO (per existing wiki source), 2026 is shaping up as the year the AI economy crosses into public markets at trillion-dollar scale. Anthropic + SpaceX in the same quarter is an unprecedented cap-table expansion.
2. **AI-bubble debate intensifies**: The NYT (2026-05-29) headline "Sky-High I.P.O. Pricing Isn't Great for Real People" frames the public reception. MarketWise (2026-05-26) calls the SpaceX-Anthropic wave "The AI IPO Trap That Already Crushed Figma" — referencing Figma's post-IPO correction. The $1T valuation is a marker of late-cycle exuberance.
3. **Anthropic's warnings on secondary markets**: Anthropic has been vocal (Axios 2026-05-13, TechCrunch 2026-05-12) about unauthorized secondary platforms offering access to its shares — suggesting demand is so high the company is worried about retail bagholders via unregulated channels.
4. **OpenAI competitive context**: Anthropic's IPO filing puts pressure on OpenAI, which has been the AI-front-runner. If Anthropic prices successfully, it becomes a publicly-comparable company, narrowing OpenAI's fundraising leverage.
