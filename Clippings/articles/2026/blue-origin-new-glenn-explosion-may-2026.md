# Blue Origin Faces Months of Delays After Rocket Explosion

**Update (May 30)**: The May 29 New Glenn rocket explosion has caused substantial damage to the Cape Canaveral launch pad, with Blue Origin facing months of delays according to Reuters reporting.

**Why it matters**: NASA's lunar mission timeline depends on Blue Origin's Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) deliveries. The explosion casts doubt on NASA's Moon plans — per prior coverage (May 29). This is a material escalation from the initial explosion report.

**Key facts**:
- Rocket: New Glenn (Blue Origin)
- Incident: Explosion on launch pad, Cape Canaveral
- Impact: Launch pad damaged, months of delays confirmed
- Downstream: NASA Moon mission timeline affected
