# Colombia Election: De la Espriella Leads First Round, Runoff Against Cepeda Set — June 2026

## What Happened

Colombia's presidential election on **May 31, 2026** (results continuing to be reported through June 1) advances to a runoff between:

- **Abelardo de la Espriella** — right-wing, Bukele-inspired, openly pro-Trump
- **Iván Cepeda** — leftist senator, ally of outgoing President Gustavo Petro

The Guardian reports: "Colombia's far-right presidential candidate De la Espriella wins first round of vote ahead of runoff." PBS: "Polls close in Colombia vote with Espriella and Cepeda advancing to runoff." NYT: "Colombia Presidential Election Heads to a Runoff."

The Petro-aligned ruling party has reportedly begun "sowing doubt in results" per AP News.

**Markets**: Bloomberg reports "Colombian Assets Surge as Right-Wing Outsider Leads Vote" — peso and equities reacting positively to the right-wing lead.

## Significance

- **Ideological polarity** — Bukele-model authoritarian-right vs. Petro-model left-populist. Outcome will define Latin America's third-largest economy (after Brazil, Mexico) for 4 years
- **US-Colombia relations** — De la Espriella is openly pro-Trump; Cepeda represents continued Petro-era tensions with Washington. A De la Espriella win would reset US-Colombia relations
- **Markets are pricing in right-wing victory** — peso and asset surge suggests investors view Espriella as more market-friendly than Cepeda
- **Runoff dynamics** — first-round winner advantage is significant but not decisive; Cepeda will try to consolidate center-left/anti-Espriella vote

## Context

- Colombia is a NATO global partner, key US counter-narcotics ally, and major cocaine source
- Petro's government strained relations with Trump admin over deportation flights, drug policy
- Political violence shadowed the campaign (per Guardian prior reporting)
- Latin America trend: hard-right victories in Argentina (Milei), El Salvador (Bukele), Ecuador (Millei-influenced Noboa); Colombia would extend the pattern
