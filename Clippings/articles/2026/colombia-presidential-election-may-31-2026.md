# Colombia Votes in Pivotal Presidential Election — US Relations at Stake

## What Happened
- **May 31, 2026**: Colombians went to the polls in a presidential election that could significantly redefine relations with the United States.
- The election pits the **outgoing leftist President Petro's ally** against **pro-Trump, right-leaning candidates**.
- Colombia has been a key US ally in Latin America but under Petro pursued more independent, leftist policies including relations with Venezuela and Cuba.

## Stakes
- A victory for Petro's successor would likely continue the current trajectory of more autonomous Latin American foreign policy.
- A pro-Trump candidate would align Colombia more closely with Washington on trade, security, and drug policy.
- Colombia is the US's oldest democratic ally in Latin America; the relationship under Petro has been tested.
- The US has been pressing for cooperation on drug trafficking and immigration.

## Context
- The election is being held amid a resurgence of political violence in some regions.
- Regional trend: Latin America has been shifting left in recent years, with several countries electing progressive governments.
- Trump administration has been actively courting right-leaning Latin American governments.
