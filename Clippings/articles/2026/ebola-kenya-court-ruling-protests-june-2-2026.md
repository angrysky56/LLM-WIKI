# Kenya Court Rules Against US Ebola Quarantine Center — 2 Killed in Protests

**Date**: 2026-06-02
**Source cluster**: BBC, NYT, Al Jazeera, Reuters, Washington Post, ABC News, CNN, PBS

## What Happened

- **2 people shot dead** in Kenya protests against the US Ebola quarantine center plan (BBC, 2026-06-02 10:53 GMT)
- **High Court orders Kenya government to release details of Ebola facility** (Al Jazeera, 2026-06-02 09:20 GMT)
- **NYT** (2026-06-02 12:20 GMT): "U.S. Ebola Unit Plans in Kenya, Subject of Protests, Suffers New Setback From Court Ruling"
- **Reuters** (2026-06-02 06:42 GMT): "Protests over US Ebola site in Kenya kill two, court keeps block"

## Why It Matters

1. **Public-health geopolitics**: The US wants to use a Kenyan facility to quarantine American Ebola patients — bypassing the typical "evacuate to US/Europe" pattern. The optics (Americans treated in Africa, near the outbreak's likely spillover zone) are fueling a sovereignty-and-racial-justice backlash.
2. **DRC outbreak worsening**: This is happening while the DRC outbreak continues to grow (WHO calling for community cooperation, 3 vaccines in development per BBC). The Kenya facility would be a regional overflow site.
3. **Sovereignty precedent**: The Kenyan court's decision to keep the block (after the initial May 29 ruling) is a test case for African sovereignty over US-dictated health infrastructure. If the Kenyan government backs down under US pressure, the precedent is set; if it holds, the African-Loss-of-Sovereignty narrative gains ground.
4. **Risk of unrest**: Two people shot dead in protests is significant. Kenya has not seen this level of anti-US-health-infrastructure violence before. Risk of further escalation if the US doubles down.
