# Ebola spread in central Africa could match 2014 record outbreak, US health officials say

**Date**: June 6, 2026
**Sources**: Guardian, NYT

US health officials have warned that the ongoing Ebola outbreak in central Africa could match the scale of the 2014 record outbreak. The warning comes as multiple factors compound the crisis:

On-the-ground challenges:
• Rebel attacks in eastern DRC have killed 30 people and are hampering Ebola response efforts
• Gold mines have emerged as transmission hubs, where miners work in close quarters across borders
• An American-only Ebola quarantine centre in Kenya has been criticized by experts as counterproductive
• WHO officials are investigating whether China will step up its response as the outbreak spreads in East Africa

The WHO International Health Regulations Emergency Committee, which met in May 2026 regarding Ebola, faces renewed pressure as case counts accelerate.
