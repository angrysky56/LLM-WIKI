# Ghana Parliament Passes Anti-LGBT Bill

Ghana's parliament passed a bill criminalizing gay acts on May 30, 2026. The legislation awaits presidential signature to become law. The bill introduces criminal penalties for "gay acts" — broadly defined — with imprisonment terms for individuals convicted under the law.

**Why it matters**: Ghana is one of Africa's more stable democracies. This legislative action puts it in tension with Western diplomatic partners and international human rights frameworks. The president's signature decision will be closely watched.

**Key facts**:
- Parliament passed: May 30, 2026
- Status: Awaiting presidential signature
- Penalty: Imprisonment for gay acts
- International context: Several African nations have similar laws; Ghana's passage contrasts with regional trends in South Africa where gay rights are protected
