# Hezbollah Rejects Lebanon-Israel Ceasefire Deal

(BBC, NYT, NPR, AP, DW — June 5, 2026)

Hezbollah has explicitly rejected the ceasefire agreement announced Wednesday night by the United States following fresh rounds of trilateral talks between the US, Israel, and Lebanon. The deal required Hezbollah to stop firing first — but the group, not party to the talks, rejected the conditions. Israeli strikes continued, killing at least 4 in south Lebanon. The rejection reveals a fundamental structural flaw: Hezbollah, the armed non-state actor that controls south Lebanon, was not at the table.
