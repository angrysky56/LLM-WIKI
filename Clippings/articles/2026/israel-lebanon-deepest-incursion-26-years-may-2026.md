# Israel Seizes Beaufort Castle — Deepest Lebanon Incursion in 26 Years

## What Happened
- **May 31, 2026**: Israeli forces crossed the Litani River in southern Lebanon, seizing the 12th-century Beaufort Castle (also called Beaufort Castle/قلعة Beaufort).
- This represents the **deepest Israeli ground incursion into Lebanon since the 2000 withdrawal** — approximately 26 years.
- The Israeli army announced a "large-scale" offensive in southern Lebanon. IDF raised the flag over the captured castle.
- **Netanyahu**: Vowed to expand Israel's grip on Lebanon. "We will expand our grip as needed."

## Context
- This occurs during **Iran War Day 93** — Israel is conducting a multi-front expansion while the US-Iran deal remains in limbo.
- Israel has also issued additional displacement orders in Lebanon and seized other strategic positions.
- Hezbollah has been conducting strikes on northern Israeli beaches, causing panic among residents.

## Significance
- The Litani River is a recognized humanitarian line — crossing it with ground forces marks a significant escalation.
- Beaufort Castle is a strategic hilltop fortress controlling the eastern slope of the Lebanese foothills.
- This is the most significant territorial expansion since the initial Lebanon invasion phase.
