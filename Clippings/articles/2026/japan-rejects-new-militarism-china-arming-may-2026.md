# Japan Rebukes China Over "New Militarism" Label — Defense Spending Spat Escalates

## What Happened
- **May 31, 2026**: Japan's defense minister publicly rejected China's characterization of Japan as pursuing "new militarism," labeling China's own military buildup as the real concern.
- Japan is accelerating defense spending increases (targeting 2% of GDP via NATO methodology) and expanding its counterstrike capabilities.
- China has been publicly amplifying the "new militarism" framing against Japan in international forums.

## Key Quotes
- **Japan defense minister**: Denied militarism claims, criticized China's "huge arsenal," called for candid dialog.
- China has been rapidly arming with defense spending growth outpacing regional neighbors, with a naval fleet now larger than the US Navy.

## Significance
- Represents a new level of direct, public rhetorical escalation between Tokyo and Beijing.
- Japan is undergoing its most significant defense policy transformation since WWII — loosening constraints on collective self-defense, increasing defense budgets, developing strike capabilities.
- China is actively trying to internationally isolate Japan narratively while expanding its own presence in the East China Sea.
