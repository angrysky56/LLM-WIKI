# Quantinuum Upsizes US IPO to $1.46B at $14.3B Valuation — June 1, 2026

## What Happened

Honeywell-backed **Quantinuum** upsized its US IPO, targeting to raise up to **$1.46 billion** at a valuation of **$14.3 billion**, per Bloomberg, Reuters, MarketScreener, and TradingView reporting on June 1, 2026.

## Significance

- **Quantum-computing IPO milestone** — Quantinuum is a major pure-play trapped-ion quantum hardware company. A $14.3B valuation makes it one of the largest publicly traded quantum companies
- **Honeywell strategic positioning** — Honeywell remains a major backer, having merged its quantum division with Cambridge Quantum in 2021 to form Quantinuum
- **Public market validation for quantum hardware** — following IonQ, Rigetti, and D-Wave listings, this is another data point on whether public markets will fund quantum hardware at scale
- **Timing matters** — comes amid AI-bubble debate (Bloomberg separately reporting JPMorgan traders casting doubt on speculative tech after 57% rally). Quantum is being floated as a "next-AI" thesis

## Context

- Quantum-computing startups have struggled post-2022 in private markets; recent re-acceleration of public offerings suggests renewed investor appetite
- Competing architectures: trapped ion (Quantinuum, IonQ), superconducting (Google, IBM, Rigetti), neutral atom (QuEra, Atom Computing), photonic (PsiQuantum)
- Q1 2026: multiple quantum-computing news milestones including error-correction breakthroughs and Microsoft's Majorana 1 progress
