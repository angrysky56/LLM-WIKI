# Ukraine Drone Explosion at Romanian Port + Zelensky Peace Offer

(BBC, Al Jazeera — June 5, 2026)

A malfunctioning Ukrainian naval drone caused an explosion at a key Black Sea port in Romania. EC President called it a "direct consequence" of the Russia-Ukraine war — the first confirmed war spillover into NATO territory via a Ukrainian-operated munition. Separately, Ukraine struck five ships in the Sea of Azov. Zelensky published an open letter to Putin proposing face-to-face talks, arguing only "direct engagement" could end the war given US focus on Iran.
