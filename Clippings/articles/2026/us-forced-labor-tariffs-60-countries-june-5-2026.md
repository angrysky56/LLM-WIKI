# US Proposes Sweeping Forced-Labor Tariffs on 60 Economies

(NYT, WSJ, CNBC, WaPo, Reuters — June 5, 2026)

The Trump administration proposed new tariffs of 10% or more on 59 countries and the European Union, citing forced labor trade practices. The move applies to most of America's largest trading partners simultaneously — China, EU, Mexico, India, Japan, South Korea, Vietnam, and dozens more. If implemented, this would be the broadest trade action in modern US history, coming on top of existing US-China tariffs, Brazil 25% tariff, and USMCA renewal negotiations.
