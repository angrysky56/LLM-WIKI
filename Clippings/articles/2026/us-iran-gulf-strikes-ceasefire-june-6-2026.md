# US and Iran exchange strikes in Gulf in latest test of ceasefire

**Date**: June 6, 2026
**Sources**: BBC World

The United States and Iran exchanged strikes in the Persian Gulf on June 6, 2026, in what analysts describe as the latest test of the fragile ceasefire between the two nations.

Key context:
• The exchange follows months of escalating tensions in Gulf waters
• Iran's World Cup team visa dispute with the US adds diplomatic dimension to military friction
• Container shipping rates from Asia to the US have spiked 109% since the Iran war began (Bloomberg)

The strikes come after the US House voted to halt Iran war powers earlier in June, suggesting continued tension between Congressional constraints on executive military action and the reality of ongoing hostilities in the Gulf.
