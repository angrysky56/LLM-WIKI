# US and Iran Trade Strikes as Ceasefire Talks Continue — June 1, 2026

## What Happened

The United States bombed Iranian military sites including radar installations and air defenses, while Iran retaliated by targeting American forces stationed in Kuwait with drones and missiles, in a major escalation of the war now in its 94th day.

**US action**: US forces struck Iranian radar sites and air-defense positions. The WSJ reported the strikes hit "Iranian Air Defenses, Drone Site in New Hormuz Skirmish."

**Iranian retaliation**: Kuwait reported missile and drone attacks on its territory targeting US military positions. Kuwait publicly condemned the Iranian attack. Per BBC reporting, Iran has damaged "20 US military sites since start of war" per satellite image analysis.

## Significance

- **Direct US-Iran combat exchange** — not proxy, not via Iraqi/Kurdish territory; Iran is striking US assets in Kuwait, a Gulf state hosting US Central Command forward operating bases
- **Happens parallel to ceasefire talks** — Trump administration was reportedly editing a draft US-Iran agreement including provisions on enriched uranium and Strait of Hormuz (per CBS News source). Talks vs. military operations now visibly co-occurring
- **Hormuz dimension** — strikes around the Strait come as the US and Iran negotiate over shipping access. A deal that reopens Hormuz was previously a Trump "condition"
- **Kuwait as theater** — first time Kuwait has been visibly drawn in as a target since the 1991 Gulf War. Kuwaiti government condemnation is a diplomatic marker
