# US Jobs Data Triggers Full Fed Rate Hike Pricing

(Bloomberg — June 5, 2026)

The US May employment report came in stronger than anticipated. Traders in the $31 trillion Treasuries market fully priced in a Federal Reserve interest-rate hike by the end of this year — the first complete rate-hike pricing since the Fed's extended pause. The S&P 500's record weekly win streak was in danger as a rotation out of AI stocks continued. European gas prices headed for a weekly gain on US-Iran deal uncertainty.
