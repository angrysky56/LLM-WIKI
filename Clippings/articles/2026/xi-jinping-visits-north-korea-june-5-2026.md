# Xi Jinping to Meet Kim Jong Un in Rare North Korea Visit

(BBC, New York Times, Al Jazeera — June 5, 2026)

China's Xi Jinping is set to visit North Korea for a rare summit with Kim Jong Un, taking place weeks after Xi met the leaders of the US and Russia. The visit comes one day after Kim unveiled a new uranium-enrichment facility and called for "exponential" nuclear arsenal expansion. This is Xi's first DPRK visit after meeting both Trump and Putin, positioning China as the central broker between three nuclear-armed states. The visit signals Xi's attempt to reassert influence over Pyongyang after Russia reduced Kim's dependence on China.
