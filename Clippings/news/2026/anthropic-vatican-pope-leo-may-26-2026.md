# Why the Vatican Invited Anthropic to the Pope's AI Encyclical Presentation

**Source**: WIRED, May 26, 2026

## Summary

Pope Leo XIV invited Anthropic to present at the May 26 rollout of his encyclical "Magnifica humanitas" — making Anthropic the only major AI company publicly sharing a platform with the Vatican at the encyclical's debut. Anthropic co-founder highlighted three ethical challenges of AI during the presentation. The Washington Post separately reported that Anthropic "aligns with Vatican over White House" — positioning the company explicitly on the opposing side of the Trump administration's AI approach.

## Key Facts

- Anthropic is the only major AI company with a reported formal role in the Vatican's encyclical presentation
- At the event, Anthropic's co-founder identified three ethical challenges of AI
- The Trump administration has split internal reactions to the encyclical (per CNBCreporting May 26)
- Vance (VP) publicly called the encyclical "profound"
- Microsoft, Google, and OpenAI did not have a reported formal role in the event

## Significance

Anthropic is explicitly choosing the Vatican as a counter-weight to the US government's AI posture — a striking move for a US-based AI company. The encyclical creates a new geopolitical actor (the Vatican with 1.4B Catholics, 183-country diplomatic network) on AI governance, and Anthropic positioning itself alongside it represents a realignment of AI company diplomacy. This complicates the US government's efforts to shape global AI rules, particularly given that Anthropic's geopolitical alignment differs from the current White House approach.
