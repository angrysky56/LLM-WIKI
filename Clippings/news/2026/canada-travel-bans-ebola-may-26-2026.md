# Canada Imposes Ebola Travel Bans — Global Entry Restrictions Multiply

**Sources**: Reuters, CBC, CTV News, The Hindu — May 26–27, 2026

## Summary

Canada has suspended visas and imposed travel restrictions on nationals from DRC, Uganda, and South Sudan effective May 26, citing the ongoing Bundibugyo Ebola outbreak. Travellers from affected nations must self-isolate upon arrival. The Bahamas has announced similar moves and plans increased screening at points of entry. Thailand has ordered 21-day quarantine for visitors from Congo and Uganda. India has implemented enhanced airport screening with flight disruption risks. ICAO has issued urgent WHO health guidance urging airlines to comply.

Separately, DRC footballers have triggered World Cup ticket refund requests as travel restrictions disrupt qualification preparations.

## Key Facts

- **Canada**: Suspended travel documents for DRC, Uganda, South Sudan; includes visa suspension and mandatory self-isolation for travellers from those countries
- **Bahamas**: Plans similar entry restrictions, increased screening
- **Thailand**: 21-day quarantine mandatory for arrivals from DRC and Uganda
- **India**: Enhanced Ebola screening at airports with potential flight cancellations
- **ICAO**: Urgent WHO health guidance issued to airlines globally
- **DRC World Cup**: Football governing bodies processing refund requests amid travel chaos

## Significance

This represents a significant global travel restriction escalation. The coordinated nature across Canada, Bahamas, Thailand, and India — within 48 hours of each other — reflects the seriousness with which countries are treating the outbreak, partly driven by the WHO PHEIC declaration. The World Cup connection adds political pressure on DRC's qualification standing and creates a financial impact beyond health.
