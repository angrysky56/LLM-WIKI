# CBP Certifies $85B in Tariff Refunds — $20.6B To Importers

**Source**: Supply Chain Dive, IndexBox, The Economic Times — May 26, 2026

## Summary

US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has certified $85 billion in tariff refunds as of May 22, 2026, with $20.6 billion in certified refunds currently in the pipeline to go to US importers. The refunds are a direct consequence of the Supreme Court's ruling that invalidated the Trump's administration's use of IEEPA for broad tariff impositions. Importers who paid tariffs under the disputed authority are now receiving refunds through a CBP certification process.

## Key Facts

- **$85B total refunds processed** by CBP as of May 22
- **$20.6B certified refunds** in active pipeline to importers
- **CBIZ advises importers to act now** — the IEEPA refund window has specific filing deadlines
- Small businesses particularly at risk of missing deadlines (Detroit News noted Michigan small businesses coverage gap)
- The refunds directly reflect the Supreme Court's ruling on US tariff authority limitations

## Significance

This is the operational economic consequence of the Supreme Court's tariff authority ruling. It demonstrates that the Court's decision has produced a real $85 billion flux in trade finances — back to importers. The window for filing is closing soon, which means the practical impact of the Court's decision is playing out right now, with or without fresh new tariff authority legislation. This also signals that the administration is processing the ruling (issuing refunds) rather than disputing it, which is notable given the context of other executive actions.

## Context

For the EU-US deal and bilateral trade negotiations, this refund process is the backdrop: the US has been paying back billions that were collected under contested authority. This creates negotiating leverage for other countries (the EU, India, ASEAN) who can point to the Supreme Court's ruling as establishing the limits of US unilateral tariff action.
