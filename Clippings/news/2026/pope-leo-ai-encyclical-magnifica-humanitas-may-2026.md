# Pope Leo XIV Presents "Magnifica humanitas" — First AI Encyclical (May 27, 2026)

## Overview

Pope Leo XIV presented his first encyclical, **"Magnifica humanitas"** (Magnificent Humanity), on May 27, 2026 — marking the first time a papal encyclical's primary subject is artificial intelligence. The document runs approximately 42,300 words and calls for binding international treaties for AI arms control, labeling AI development without constraints as a "technologic threat to humanity."

## Key Elements

- **"Disarm AI" call**: The encyclical explicitly calls on world governments to pursue AI disarmament — framing AI arms control similarly to nuclear disarmament negotiations
- **Anthropic co-presentation**: Anthropic co-presented at the Vatican rollout — the only major AI company present. Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI did not attend. This is significant as Anthropic was the only AI company visibly aligning with the Vatican over the US government's AI posture.
- **Vatican diplomatic machinery**: The Vatican has a 1.4 billion-person constituency and a 183-country diplomatic network. This encyclical operationalizes the Vatican as a global AI governance actor — not just moral voice.
- **"Profound" vs. split reactions**: Vance called the encyclical "profound." Other Trump officials were split in their reactions — per earlier coverage, the administration abandoned federal AI regulation (May 22), leaving California and now the Vatican as the primary AI governance voices in the US ecosystem.

## Previous Context (from carryover)

Per carryover (May 26): Anthropic co-presented with the Vatican at the encyclical rollout — first US AI company visibly positioning against its own government's AI posture. The May 27 formal presentation confirms and extends this pattern.

## Why This Matters

1. **Governance vacuum filled**: With Trump abandoning federal AI regulation (May 22) and no US State Department guidance on Vatican AI diplomacy, the governance vacuum is being filled by non-state actors — the Vatican and California.
2. **Anthropic's strategic positioning**: Anthropic is the only AI company that showed up. This is a deliberate geopolitical bet — positioning the company with a 1.4B-person constituency and 183-country diplomatic network over the current US administration.
3. **Encyclical as soft power**: The Vatican's 42,300-word document is a significant soft power move. It sets the terms of the AI governance debate in a way that will influence global opinion — particularly in the Global South, where the Vatican has strong relationships.
