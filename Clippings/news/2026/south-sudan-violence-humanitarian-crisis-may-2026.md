# South Sudan Violence Escalation — May 2026

## Key Events

### Civilians Killed While Fleeing (May 2026)

Doctors Without Borders (MSF) reported that civilians in South Sudan were killed while running from attacks. The humanitarian situation has deteriorated significantly — the UN and MSF both report mass atrocities including killings, rape, and deliberate attacks on healthcare infrastructure.

### UN Joint Declaration on Sudan (May 12)

The African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights and the UN Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan jointly adopted a declaration on the human rights situation in Sudan. This signals increasing international attention to the conflict.

### Hunger as Weapon of War

The Guardian (May 24) reported that hunger is increasingly used as a weapon of war in the region — "food-related violence" surging. This is a pattern consistent with the broader escalation of the Sudan/South Sudan conflict.

## Why This Matters

South Sudan is not the primary Ebola outbreak zone (the DRC/Uganda focus is), but the violence is directly relevant because:
1. **Refugee/drone flows**: Violence forces population movement toward Ebola-affected zones — complicates containment
2. **Healthcare infrastructure**: Attacks on healthcare facilities reduce capacity to respond to Ebola
3. **Cross-border transmission**: Any South Sudan cases would represent spread beyond the current geographic focus
