## Title:Novel H^\\mathrm{dev}(\\mathrm{Curl})-conforming elements on regular triangulations and Clough--Tocher splits for the planar relaxed micromorphic model

[View PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.14849) [HTML (experimental)](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14849v1)

> Abstract:In this work we present a consistent reduction of the relaxed micromorphic model to its corresponding two-dimensional planar model, such that its capacity to capture discontinuous dilatation fields is preserved. As a direct consequence of our approach, new conforming finite elements for $H^\mathrm{dev}(\mathrm{Curl},A)$ become necessary. We present two novel $H^\mathrm{dev}(\mathrm{Curl},A)$ -conforming finite element spaces, of which one is a macro element based on Clough--Tocher splits, as well as primal and mixed variational formulations of the planar relaxed micromorphic model. Finally, we demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach with two numerical examples.
