# AI Governance

## Overview

The global AI governance landscape as of May 2026 is characterized by: EU AI Act implementation underway, **US federal AI regulation definitively off the table** (Trump abandoned pre-release model vetting before signing, May 22), California functioning as *de facto* US AI regulator, and Pope Leo XIV entering the governance debate with *Magnifica humanitas* encyclical demanding binding AI disarmament treaties (May 26).

## Key Developments

### US Federal Retreat (May 22)
Trump administration abandoned pre-release vetting of AI models from major labs (Google, Microsoft, xAI) before signing — second reversal in one week. Federal AI regulation definitively off table. California (May 21 AI worker protection order) is now the primary US AI governance actor.

### Vatican Entry (May 26)
Pope Leo XIV released *Magnifica humanitas* — first papal encyclical on AI as primary subject. Frames AI as arms-control/disarmament problem requiring binding international treaties. 1.4 billion Catholics, Vatican diplomatic network (183 countries, UN observer status) makes this a qualitatively new global governance voice.
