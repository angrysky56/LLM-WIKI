# Allometric Scaling

Allometric scaling describes the proportional relationship between the size of an organism and its biological traits. Famous examples:

- **Kleiber's law** — metabolic rate scales as mass^0.75
- **Brain-body scaling** — brain size increases slower than body size
- **Heart rate** — larger animals have slower heart rates

In AI, allometric thinking appears in:
- [[taylors-law]] — ecological application
- Power-law scaling of neural network components
- Circuit complexity scaling in biology
