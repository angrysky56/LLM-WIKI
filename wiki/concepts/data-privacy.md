# Data Privacy

Data privacy in AI systems concerns the collection, processing, storage, and use of personal information. Key frameworks:

- **Differential privacy** — mathematical guarantee of privacy via noise injection
- **Federated learning** — training on decentralized data without raw data transfer
- **Data minimization** — collecting only what's necessary
- **Purpose limitation** — using data only for stated purposes

Privacy-preserving techniques in AI:
- [[federated-learning]] — decentralized training
- [[privacy-utility-tradeoff]] — balancing privacy with model quality
- Secure multi-party computation
- Homomorphic encryption
