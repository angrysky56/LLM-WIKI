# Ebola Outbreak DRC 2026

## Overview

Bundibugyo Ebola virus disease outbreak in eastern DRC (Ituri/Nord-Kivu/Sud-Kivu provinces) with cross-border transmission to Uganda. Declared a **Public Health Emergency of International Concern (PHEIC)** by WHO Director-General on May 17, 2026 — first such declaration under IHR (2005) for Bundibugyo strain. WHO IHR Emergency Committee convened May 19, issued temporary recommendations May 22, 2026. Outbreak occurs in active conflict zone (Ituri province, M23/rebel-held territory), making containment exceptionally difficult.

## Key Facts

- **Strain:** Bundibugyo (BDBV) — no approved vaccine or treatment at time of outbreak (unlike Zaire ebolavirus, for which rVSV-ZEBOV/Merck vaccine exists)
- **Scale (May 22, WHO DON603):** 746 suspected + 85 confirmed = ~831 total cases across both countries; 176+10 = 186 deaths
- **Scale (May 26, CDC):** DRC: 906 suspected + 105 confirmed = 1,011 total; 223 suspected + 10 confirmed = 233 deaths. Uganda: 7 confirmed, 1 death. **Grand total: ~1,018+ cases, 244+ deaths**
- **Scale (May 26 WSWS report):** 1,005 cases, 230 deaths (DRC figures, likely earlier snapshot)
- **Sud-Kivu confirmed case** (first: May 26 CDC) — outbreak no longer confined to Ituri and Nord-Kivu; geographic expansion ongoing
- **Major urban centers infected:** Bunia and Goma now have confirmed cases
- **CFR:** 11-12% among confirmed cases; 25-50% historically for Bundibugyo
- **WHO Director-General** (May 22 briefing): outbreak is "outpacing us"; WHO revised risk upward: "very high" (national DRC), "high" (regional Africa), "low" (global)
- **Imperial College London modeling** (Ruth McCabe et al., May 2026): true infections 2x+ above official counts in early weeks due to detection gaps in conflict zones

## ETC Attacks — Confirmed Pattern

The concept of "ETC attacks driving patients into rebel-held territory" is confirmed:
- **May 21:** Youths in Rwampara set fire to Ebola treatment center after police blocked retrieval of a friend's body for traditional burial. Police fired warning shots; facility burned; aid workers fled.
- **May 23:** Residents in Mongbwalu set fire to a Doctors Without Borders-operated treatment center — **second attack in 48 hours**. Eighteen (18) suspected Ebola patients **fled into the surrounding community**. This is the direct mechanism by which official counts systematically under-represent actual spread.
- **May 26:** Attack on third ETC confirmed in Mongbwalu — escalation of attack frequency
- **Pattern:** Attack frequency roughly every 2-3 days suggests a cycle. Next elevated window: **May 29-30** based on this pattern, though no specific confirmed threat.

## Treatment / Therapeutic Pipeline

- **No licensed Bundibugyo vaccine exists.** rVSV-ZEBOV (Merck) does not cross-protect. Ring vaccination with experimental stocks is the only available tool.
- **NV-387 (NanoViricides):** Oral broad-spectrum antiviral; company issued press release May 26 stating it "compares favorably as a treatment for Ebola versus possible options." **Pipeline candidate only — not approved, not Emergency Use Authorized.**
- **Thermostable vaccine:** Flagged in earlier reporting as potentially significant for conflict-zone cold-chain limitations. WHO EC temporary recommendations called for accelerated R&D. **Deployment decision pending; no confirmed deployment as of May 26/27.**

## International Response

- **USAID funding cuts** (83% of programs terminated in 2025): Decimated local health partners; ICRC warned health system in North/South Kivu "on the verge of collapse" (June 2025). Contributing structural factor.
- **USAID Lancet study (2025):** Projected 14M+ additional deaths globally by 2030 from USAID cuts, including 4.5M+ among children under 5.
- **ICD / ICRC 2025 assessment:** 109 health centers surveyed in North/South Kivu — 50% drop in under-5 medical visits; 3 of 5 facilities looted; 4x increase in stillbirths.
- **WHO response:** 17+ tons of emergency supplies shipped; EpiShuttle isolation transport deployed; helicopter/armored vehicles mobilised; genomic sequencing uploaded to virological.org.

## IHR EC Temporary Recommendations (May 22) — Key Points

1. **Coordinate:** Declare national health emergency; activate emergency operations centers; establish security corridors for responders
2. **Surveillance:** Strengthen early detection; 21-day contact follow-up; decentralize PCR laboratory capacity (GeneXpert platform cannot detect BDBV — noted specifically)
3. **IPC:** Systematic health facility mapping; continuous PPE training; health worker exposure protocols
4. **Safe burials:** Community-participatory protocols; family presence provisions; non-coercive approach
5. **Border health:** Exit screening at PoEs; no travel restrictions recommended; no border closures
6. **R&D:** Urgent validation of PCR diagnostic platforms; ethically approved clinical trials for candidate therapeutics and vaccines
7. **For bordering countries:** Active surveillance; laboratory access; cross-border contact tracing coordination

**Gap:** Recommendations assume a functioning state health system — conflict zone reality (inaccessibility, community distrust of police-enforced burial, armed actors controlling territory) makes full implementation impossible.

## Diplomatic and Operational Footprint

- **India-Africa Forum Summit** postponed (May 22) — diplomatic consequence of PHEEC declaration
- **India travel advisory** (May 24): Against non-essential travel to DRC, Uganda, and South Sudan
- **PAHO:** Reinforced preparedness across the Americas anticipating imported cases
- **Barrick Gold (Kibali mine, DRC):** Intensified Ebola screening at operations; corporate operational continuity risk
