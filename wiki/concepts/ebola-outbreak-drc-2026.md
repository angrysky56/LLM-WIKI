# Ebola Outbreak DRC 2026

## Overview

Bundibugyo Ebola virus disease outbreak declared a PHEIC, concentrated in eastern DRC (Ituri province, rebel-held territory) with cross-border transmission to Uganda. WHO IHR Emergency Committee convened and issued temporary recommendations May 22, 2026.

## Key Facts

- **Strain:** Bundibugyo — no approved vaccine or treatment at time of outbreak (unlike Zaire ebolavirus)
- **Scale:** ~750 suspected cases, 177+ deaths (CIDRAP, May 22); WHO DG called outbreak "outpacing us"
- **Risk:** Area risk upgraded to "very high" 
- **Governance:** WHO IHR EC temporary recommendations issued May 22 — border screening, vaccine/therapeutic sharing, conflict-zone surveillance
- **Thermostable vaccine tech** flagged as potential deployment (critical for conflict-zone cold-chain limitations)
- **Key vulnerability:** 18+ patients fled into rebel-held territory after ETC attacks on May 20/23/26 — official counts systematically under-represent actual spread
