# Governance

AI governance encompasses the structures, rules, and processes by which AI systems are directed, monitored, and controlled. It addresses:

- **Alignment** — ensuring AI pursuits human-intended goals
- **Oversight** — mechanisms for humans to supervise AI behavior
- **Accountability** — assigning responsibility for AI actions
- **Transparency** — making AI decision-making understandable
