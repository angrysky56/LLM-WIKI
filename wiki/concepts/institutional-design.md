# Institutional Design

The study and practice of designing organizations and structures that produce desired governance outcomes. In AI context, institutional design addresses:

- **Incentive structures** — how to design rewards and penalties
- **Monitoring mechanisms** — how to observe and verify behavior
- **Constraint systems** — how to bound permissible actions
- **Exit and voice** — how participants can challenge or leave institutions
