# Mathematical Reasoning

Mathematical reasoning encompasses the methods and principles of rigorous deduction in mathematics, including:

- **Deductive reasoning** — deriving conclusions from premises via logical inference
- **Proof techniques** — direct, contrapositive, induction, contradiction
- **Abstraction** — identifying common structural patterns across domains
- **Formalization** — encoding mathematical statements in formal languages
