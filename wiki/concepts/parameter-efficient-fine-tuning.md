# Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

> Stub — expand with full content.

**PEFT methods** reduce computational cost of fine-tuning large models by updating only a small subset of parameters. Key approaches include:

- **LoRA** (Hu et al., 2021): Low-rank adaptation via pair of trainable rank-decomposition matrices
- **QLoRA** (Dettmers et al., 2023): Quantized LoRA for 4-bit base model fine-tuning
- **AdaLoRA** (Zhang et al., 2023): Adaptive rank allocation based on importance
