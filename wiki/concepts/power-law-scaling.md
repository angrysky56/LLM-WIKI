# Power Law Scaling

Power law scaling describes how properties of a system change with its size according to a power law relationship. In AI:

- **Neural scaling laws** — loss decreases as a power of model size, data, or compute
- **Emergent abilities** — capabilities appear suddenly at threshold scales
- **Allometric scaling** — biological analogs in neural systems

The neural scaling law empirical finding:
```
L(N) ∝ N^(-α)  where α ≈ 0.05-0.1
```

For compute-optimal scaling, Kaplan et al. found different exponents for different resources.
