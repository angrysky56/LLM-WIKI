# Proof Assistant

A proof assistant (interactive theorem prover) is software that helps humans construct and verify mathematical proofs. Leading examples include:

- **Coq** — based on the Calculus of Inductive Constructions
- **Isabelle** — generic proof assistant used in the seL4 verification
- **Lean** — modern proof assistant with an active ecosystem
- **Agda** — dependently typed programming language and proof assistant

Proof assistants have been used to verify:
- The four-color theorem
- The seL4 microkernel
- The CompCert verified C compiler
