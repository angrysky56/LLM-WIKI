# Proxy Signalling

Proxy signalling is an indirect form of strategic communication where a power demonstrates capability or resolve through actions in third-party contexts rather than direct confrontation.

Forms:
- **Proxy support** — backing allies or non-state actors in conflicts
- **Base positioning** — military presence in strategically located states
- **Diplomatic gestures** — high-level visits, agreements in third countries
- **Economic leverage** — sanctions, trade deals in third-party contexts

The China-Cuba relationship exemplifies proxy signalling in the context of great power rivalry.
