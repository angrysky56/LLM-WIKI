# Longevity Research

Longevity research is the interdisciplinary scientific effort to understand the biological mechanisms of aging and develop interventions to extend healthy human lifespan. The field spans molecular biology, neuroscience, genetics, and computational medicine.

## Core Research Areas

| Area | Focus | Key Approaches |
|------|-------|----------------|
| **Senolytics** | Clear senescent cells | Dasatinib + quercetin, FOXO4-DRI |
| **Caloric restriction** | Nutrient sensing pathways | mTOR inhibition, AMPK activation |
| **Epigenetic reprogramming** | Restore cellular age | Yamanaka factors, partial reprogramming |
| **Telomere extension** | Chromosome end maintenance | telomerase gene therapy |
| **Hypothalamic Menin** | Aging pace setter | Menin gene therapy, D-serine supplementation |

## Key Discovery — Hypothalamic Menin Switch (2026)

The most significant 2026 breakthrough in longevity research establishes the hypothalamus as a central aging pacemaker:

- **Menin** (produced in ventromedial nucleus) suppresses neuroinflammation throughout life
- **Age-related Menin decline** → microglial activation → neuroinflammation → cognitive decline
- **Downstream effects**: bone loss, skin thinning, shortened lifespan — all downstream of Menin decline
- **Gene therapy** restoring Menin in aged mouse hypothalamus reversed aging signs
- **D-serine supplementation** improved cognition but NOT physical aging markers — cognitive decline is mechanistically separate from physical aging

This discovery places the hypothalamus as both a sensor and regulator of biological aging pace.
