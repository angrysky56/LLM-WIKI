# Sakana AI

Tokyo-based AI research lab behind [[wiki/sources/papers/acdc-llm-task-capability-coevolution-sakana|AC/DC]] and related work on evolutionary model merging and open-ended learning. Sakana AI's research direction centers on applying open-endedness principles — coevolution, quality-diversity, minimal criteria — to LLM development.

## Key papers

- **AC/DC** (arXiv 2604.14969) — coevolving LLM populations with synthetic task populations
- Prior work on evolutionary model merging (EvoMerge lineage)
