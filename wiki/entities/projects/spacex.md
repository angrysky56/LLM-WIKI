# SpaceX

SpaceX (Space Exploration Technologies) is an aerospace company founded by Elon Musk in 2002. It is the dominant commercial launch provider globally and operates Starlink, the world's largest satellite internet constellation. Post-2025, the company acquired xAI and X (formerly Twitter) and filed for IPO on Nasdaq under ticker SPCX.

## Business Lines

### Launch Services
Falcon 9 (most flown rocket in history), Falcon Heavy, and Starship — the world's largest and most powerful rocket. SpaceX holds the majority of global commercial launch market share. NASA uses Starship as the Artemis lunar lander.

### Starlink
World's largest satellite internet constellation. Deployed via next-generation Starlink satellites (Gen 3 planned, deployable via Starship's "pez dispenser" mechanism). Provides global communications infrastructure with defense and rural connectivity applications.

### Artificial Intelligence
The company's IPO filing explicitly frames AI-powered space infrastructure as a core future bet. The SEC filing disclosed "AI space data centers" as an unproven commercial line. SpaceX acquired xAI (Musk's AI company) and the Anthropic deal ($15B/year) gives xAI data access rights. IPO filing acknowledges the AI space data center bet may not be commercially viable — this risk is disclosed.

### Acquisitions (Post-2025)
- **xAI**: Musk's AI company, now integrated into SpaceX
- **X**: Twitter, now owned by SpaceX; listed in IPO as an asset with associated litigation risks

## Governance Structure

SpaceX's governance structure is the central institutional investor concern for the June 12 listing:

- **Dual-class share structure**: Musk holds ~42% economic ownership (per S-1) but controls approximately 78% of voting stock through a separate class of super-voting shares
- **Musk's concurrent roles**: CEO of SpaceX, Tesla (options), xAI, X, and DOGE (Trump administration advisory role) — creates potential governance overlaps unusual for a public company
- **SpaceX declined to clarify** pre-IPO whether the voting control structure would be maintained post-listing (24/7 Wall St., May 26)
- **Quiet period**: SEC quiet period rules apply ~40 days before listing; as of May 28 (16 days out), SpaceX is in blackout and would not be commenting publicly

This creates a **novel governance instrument at unprecedented scale**: a company with the largest IPO valuation in history, controlled ~78% by one person, with no disclosure obligation on the voting structure post-IPO.

## Legal Exposure

The IPO filing flags **$500M+ expected legal costs** including:
- Multiple lawsuits over Grok (xAI chatbot) creating sexualized deepfakes of real women and girls
- Patent infringement claims
- EU content moderation non-compliance claims
- Music copyright infringement claims
- Data breach claims

Musk's concurrent roles and the multiplicity of legal disputes against SpaceX create a compliance load that is unusual for a pre-IPO company.

## Starship V3 (Flight 12)

The first Starship Version 3 flight launched May 23, 2026 from SpaceX Starbase, Texas — successful splashdown in Indian Ocean after completing primary mission objectives. Key details:

- First launch attempt scrubbed May 21; retry May 22; launch May 23
- Scaled-up booster and upper stage with upgraded Raptor engines
- Super Heavy booster: soft splashdown (Gulf of Mexico) — no return-to-launch-site attempt
- Ship performed pez-dispenser mock satellite deployment (22 mass simulators)
- Starship re-entry test including intentional heat shield tile removal to test aerodynamic forces
- Single Raptor engine relight in space
- V3 is the production version for orbital Starlink Gen 3 missions and NASA's Artemis lunar lander
- Total Starship development spending: $15B+

**Finding:** SpaceX cannot clarify. With June 12 listing 16 days away, SpaceX is in full quiet period — any such statement would be an SEC violation.

**Resolution timeline:** The question of whether Musk's ~78% voting control persists post-IPO will be answered on June 12 when:
1. The final prospectus ( prospectus supplement) is published
