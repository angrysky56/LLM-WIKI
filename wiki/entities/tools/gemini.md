# Gemini

**Gemini** is a family of large language models developed by Google DeepMind. It is notable for its native multimodality and massive context window (up to millions of tokens).

## Usage in Research
- [[why-llms-arent-scientists-yet]] — Gemini 2.5 Pro was used for context management across the entire research pipeline due to its long-context handling.
