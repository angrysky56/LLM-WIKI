# xAI

xAI (Exogenous Artificial Intelligence) is an AI company founded by Elon Musk in July 2023. It developed the Grok chatbot and was acquired by SpaceX in 2025, integrating xAI's compute infrastructure with SpaceX's Starlink satellite network.

## Grok Chatbot

Grok is xAI's primary consumer product — a conversational AI assistant notable for:
- Real-time knowledge via Starlink-connected data centers
- "Woke" training approach (Musk's stated contrast to OpenAI/Anthropic)
- Integration with X (Twitter) platform post-acquisition
- Humor-driven interaction style (per Musk's public statements)

The chatbot has been involved in multiple lawsuits over generating sexualized deepfakes of real women and girls — the IPO filing discloses $500M+ expected legal costs from these cases.

## SpaceX Integration

Post-2025 acquisition, xAI is integrated into SpaceX as the AI division:
- **AI compute via Starlink**: xAI's distributed training runs leverage Starlink's global satellite infrastructure
- **IPO framing**: SpaceX SEC filing explicitly frames "AI space data centers" as a future commercial line — unproven but disclosed as risk
- **Anthropic deal**: $15B/year agreement gives xAI data access rights; xAI provides Starlink compute to Anthropic in exchange
- **Grok enterprise**: Corporate Grok deployments over Starlink connectivity

## Connection to Musk Portfolio

xAI is part of Musk's overlapping governance structure:
- Musk: CEO of SpaceX, xAI, Tesla, X; advisor to Trump (DOGE)
- SpaceX owns xAI and X post-2025 acquisitions
- xAI's compute strategy is integrated with Tesla's Dojo (per public statements)
