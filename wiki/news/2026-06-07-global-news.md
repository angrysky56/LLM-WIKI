### 2. ⚠️ Russian Drone Strikes Spent Nuclear Fuel Storage at Chornobyl

**Sources**: BBC

**What happened**: Ukrainian officials report a Russian drone struck a spent nuclear fuel storage facility near the Chornobyl nuclear plant. Zelensky condemned the strike as "vile" ahead of key diplomatic talks in London.

**Significance**: Any breach of Chornobyl's spent fuel storage would be a transboundary radiological event affecting Ukraine, Belarus, and Europe. The timing — immediately before Ukraine's allies meet in London — suggests an attempt to weaken Kyiv's negotiating position. Coincidentally, Ukrainian drones hit St Petersburg on the same day.
