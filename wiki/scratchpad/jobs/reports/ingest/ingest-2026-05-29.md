## Summary

**Power Sampling** — training-free inference-time reasoning via Metropolis-Hastings MCMC sampling from power distributions $p^\alpha$.

Key findings:
- Base models have latent reasoning capabilities on par with RL-posttraining
- Power sampling achieves near-RL performance (MATH500, HumanEval, GPQA) without training, verifier, or curated data
- Maintains diversity that GRPO collapses — outperforms both base model and GRPO on pass@k for k > 1
- $N_{\text{MCMC}}$ serves as test-time compute scaling knob (~8.84× token cost vs standard inference)
