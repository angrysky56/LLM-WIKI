## Finding: No Google DeepMind Response as of May 28, 2026

Google DeepMind has made **no public statement**, blog post, press release, or social media communication addressing Pope Leo XIV's encyclical "Magnifica humanitas" as of May 28, 2026 — approximately two days after the encyclical was formally presented on May 27, 2026.

## Google DeepMind's Prior Vatican Engagement

Google (not specifically DeepMind) participated in **Vatican tech events prior to Pope Leo XIV's encyclical**. In June 2025, the Pope sent an ethical AI framework message to tech executives gathering at the Vatican, and Google was present among attendees. That event predates the current encyclical and does not constitute a response to "Magnifica humanitas."

---

## Context: Why This Silence Matters

Google DeepMind's silence is notable for several reasons:

1. **Anthropic's positioning** — By co-presenting at the Vatican, Anthropic signaled alignment with the Vatican's governance framing. Google, by contrast, is absent from the most significant AI governance event since the EU AI Act.

2. **US tech industry's broader silence** — Both Google and Microsoft have declined to engage publicly with the encyclical, leaving Anthropic as the sole AI company with a documented institutional relationship with the Vatican's AI governance position.

3. **DeepMind's safety focus** — DeepMind has historically positioned itself on AI safety and governance. Its silence on the encyclical — which centers on AI arms-control and binding treaty language — could reflect internal disagreement with the disarmament framing, or deliberate diplomatic neutrality.
