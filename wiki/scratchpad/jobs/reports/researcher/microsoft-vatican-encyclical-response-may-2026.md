## Microsoft's Prior Vatican Engagement

Microsoft participated in a **different Vatican tech event in June 2025** — before Pope Leo XIV released "Magnifica humanitas." In that instance, the Pope sent an ethical AI framework message to tech executives gathering at the Vatican, and Microsoft was present among the attendees. That event predates the current encyclical and does not constitute a response to "Magnifica humanitas."

## Context

The absence of a Microsoft response is notable in context:
- **Anthropic made a deliberate strategic bet** by co-presenting at the Vatican — the only AI company to do so
- **Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI were all absent** from the May 27 Vatican presentation
- **Fast Company** (May 27) reported "mixed reception from the tech world" — suggesting internal deliberations but no public positions from Microsoft, Google, or OpenAI
- The **WIRED article** (May 28, by Daniele Polidoro) explicitly examines "Why the Vatican Invited Anthropic to the Pope's AI Encyclical Presentation" — framing Anthropic's presence as a strategic choice while other companies sat out

Microsoft's silence may reflect:
1. A calculation to avoid confrontation with the White House on AI policy (as other companies may have also made)
2. Internal deliberation still ongoing
3. A decision not to engage publicly with the Vatican's "disarm AI" framing
4. Coordination with US government position on Vatican AI diplomacy
