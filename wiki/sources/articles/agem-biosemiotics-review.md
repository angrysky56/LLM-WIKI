# AGEM Biosemiotics Review

An AGEM (Agent Group Evolving Molecular) system analysis of a biosemiotics corpus titled "The Living Sign: A Foundational Overview of Biosemiotics." The AGEM framework processes the corpus through community detection (topic modeling), formal logic verification (contradiction checking), and structural analysis.

## Key Results

- **Community Detection**: The 277-line corpus resolved into 10 semantic communities, mapping to the 7 substantive sections (Peircean triad, agency, Umwelt, Semiosphere, genetic code, paradigm-shift, negation-of-mechanism) plus 3 housekeeping clusters.
- **Bridge Analysis**: Strongest connections identified between agency ↔ Umwelt (w=118.8), Peircean sign theory ↔ paradigm-shift claim (w=97.9), and organism ↔ sign interpretation (w=89.1).
- **Weakest Link**: The genetic-code/écriture ↔ Umwelt/Semiosphere bridge (w=45) — the corpus never fully develops the connection from molecular semiosis to ecosystem-level sign space.
- **Formal Logic Verification**: All 6 internal, all 15 pairwise, and all 20 triple consistency checks returned `consistent` (H¹=0). No frustrated triples or contradictions detected.
- **Regime**: Nascent — no phase transition after single ingestion. Running another cycle without new material would degrade modularity with duplicate co-occurrences.
