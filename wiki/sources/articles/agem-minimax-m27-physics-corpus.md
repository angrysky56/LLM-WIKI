## What It Is

A single AGEM reasoning session that walks through **six major interpretations of quantum mechanics** in sequence, applying the engine's sheaf-theoretic, cohomology-based consistency analysis to each. Each interpretation is presented in two parts:

1. **The interpretation stated** — a clean, compact restatement of the doctrine
2. **A rigorous assessment** — internal architecture, strengths, vulnerabilities, cross-comparison, verdict

## The Six Interpretations Covered

| # | Interpretation | Core Thesis | Headline Vulnerability |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[[copenhagen-interpretation]]** | Wavefunction epistemic, real collapse, fundamental cut | The cut is arbitrary; collapse mechanism asserted not derived |
| 2 | **[[many-worlds-interpretation]]** | Wavefunction real, no collapse, branching on measurement | Preferred basis problem; probability's status |
| 3 | **[[bohmian-mechanics]]** | Pilot-wave hidden variables + nonlocality | Nonlocality cost; guidance equation is hidden structure |

| 6 | **[[relational-quantum-mechanics]]** | All systems observer-relative; no absolute state | Recovering intersubjectivity without collapse |

## AGEM Engine Mechanics Demonstrated

The corpus showcases the full [[agem|AGEM]] stack in action:

- **Concept community detection** — each interpretation gets a topology table (e.g., Copenhagen: 6 communities; Many-Worlds: 7 communities)
- **Bridge weight analysis** — heaviest semantic bridge per interpretation is highlighted (e.g., MWI: weight 70.5 between *quantum system* and *philosophical framework* communities)
- **Sheaf cohomology (H¹)** — measured per cycle; H¹=0 = internally coherent, H¹>0 = unresolved inconsistency
- **Verification lag (T3 TIMEOUT)** — observed in MWI cycle: 73→135 nodes, 272→665 edges, 28 unverified claims — flagged as throughput constraint, not logical failure
- **Conscience-servitor warnings** — T1 WARNING on unverified claims about collapse mechanism

## Methodological Pattern

Each interpretation analysis follows the same template:

1. **Internal Architecture** (concept graph + community structure)
2. **Where It Is Strongest** (4–5 named virtues)
3. **Where It Is Vulnerable** (4–5 named weaknesses, each with consequence)
4. **Comparison with Alternatives** (5-column table: collapse? cut? probability? wavefunction? + interpretation)
5. **Synthesized Verdict** (epistemic humility score, ontological completeness score, open question)
6. **What's Next** (epistemic posture recommendation)

This template is itself a reusable artifact — a thinking framework for evaluating any theoretical framework, not just QM interpretations.

## Cross-Cutting Observations

- **Copenhagen is "in slow dissolution"** (community cluster: slow · dissolution) — absorbed by decoherence, QBism, Zurek's existential interpretation, and operationalism
- **The strongest version of any interpretation is the minimal version** — shut up and calculate, refuse ontological commitment
- **The shared failure mode is the cut** — every interpretation that posits a quantum-classical boundary must defend *where* it goes
