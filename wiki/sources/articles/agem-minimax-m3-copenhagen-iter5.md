## The Central Finding

AGEM formally registers a **sheaf contradiction** at the bridge between Copenhagen's epistemic and pragmatic claims:

> **Coboundary 0.56, dual pressure 1.08.**

The contradiction is between:
- **Epistemic claims** — wavefunction is epistemic, no definite value before measurement, complementary properties
- **Pragmatic claims** — collapse is real and irreversible, classical description of apparatus, fundamental cut

These are coherent *within* their respective clusters but **inconsistent at the bridge** between them. The doctrine works by oscillating between treating the wavefunction as epistemic (for some purposes) and as if ontic (for others).

## Three Coherent Sub-Versions (the Reconstruction)

The analysis separates Copenhagen into three internally-consistent sub-doctrines:

1. **Minimal Copenhagen** — the practitioner creed: shut up and calculate, refuse ontological commitment
2. **Epistemic Instrumentalism** — Bohr's mature view: manifestations under specific conditions, no access to system-in-itself
3. **Strong Copenhagen (the textbook view)** — wavefunction-as-physical-collapse-device with magical cut — **flagged as incoherent**

## Topological Verdict (claim-by-claim)

## Closing Reading (synthesis)

> "Copenhagen's deepest truth is methodological: it is a *discipline of restraint* masquerading as an ontology. The cost of that masquerade is the central contradiction the sheaf enforcer has now formally registered."

Historically in **slow dissolution** — its substantive content has been absorbed by:
- **Decoherence** — provides the cut's content without requiring it to be fundamental
- **QBism** — makes epistemic stance explicit, abandons "objective state vector" claim
- **Zurek's existential interpretation** — derives classicality from environment-induced superselection without collapse
- **Operationalism** — working philosophy of those using formalism without committing to interpretation
