# Anthropic's Chris Olah on Magnifica Humanitas

Chris Olah, Anthropic co-founder, shared remarks on Pope Leo XIV's encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas* released May 25, 2026. The encyclical addresses AI governance, human dignity, and the role of technology in society.

## Key Points from Olah's Remarks

- **Alignment relevance**: The encyclical's emphasis on human agency in AI decision-making resonates with Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach
- **Dignity preservation**: The document's framing of AI as serving human flourishing aligns with Anthropic's safety-focused mission
- **Global governance signal**: The Vatican's engagement with AI ethics signals growing international consensus on AI's societal role

## Context

Pope Leo XIV (Robert Francis Prevost) issued *Magnifica Humanitas* as the first encyclical explicitly addressing artificial intelligence. The document calls for:
- AI development centered on human dignity
- International cooperation on AI governance
- Protection of vulnerable populations from AI harms

Olah's response positions Anthropic as engaging constructively with institutional AI governance frameworks.
