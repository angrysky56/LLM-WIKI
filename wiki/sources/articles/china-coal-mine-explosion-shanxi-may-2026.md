# China Coal Mine Explosion — 90 Dead in Shanxi Province

## What Happened

A gas explosion at a coal mine in China's Shanxi province has killed at least 90 people, making it the country's deadliest mining disaster in 16 years. The blast occurred at the Shanxi Pinglu Coal Mine. State media reports the death toll has continued to rise as rescue operations continue. Six national rescue teams have been dispatched to the scene.

Vice Premier Liu has called for stricter safety inspections across China's mining sector following the incident.

## Why It Matters

- **Industrial safety trajectory**: China's mining safety record has improved significantly since the 2000s, but this disaster suggests enforcement gaps remain, particularly in private and smaller mines
- **Political optics**: The incident occurs amid broader concerns about China's industrial supervision under Xi Jinping's "deeper opening-up" agenda
- **Economic context**: Shanxi is one of China's major coal provinces; the blast occurred in a region producing tens of millions of tons annually, with Beijing pushing for increased domestic coal output amid energy security concerns
