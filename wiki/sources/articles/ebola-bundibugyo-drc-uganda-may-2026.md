# Ebola Bundibugyo DRC/Uganda Outbreak — May 2026

## Key Facts

- **Strain**: Bundibugyo ebolavirus — first outbreak since 2012
- **Declaration**: DRC Ministry of Health confirmed first case May 15, 2026; WHO declared PHEIC May 17, 2026
- **Geography**: Ituri, Nord-Kivu, Sud-Kivu provinces (DRC); Kampala, Uganda (5 linked cases)
- **Case count**: >1,000 cases reported (DRC + Uganda combined); >500 suspected cases noted in early ReliefWeb report
- **Severity**: WHO raised risk to "very high"; CDC screening at airports

## Context

- This is the DRC's 17th Ebola virus disease outbreak
- No cure; Ervebo vaccine available but effectiveness against Bundibugyo strain is limited
- US funding cuts affecting response capacity (per YouTube video: "Massive U.S. Funding Cuts")
- Cross-border transmission to Uganda through Kampala — unlinked imported cases
- Italy reported 2 suspected cases (imported)
