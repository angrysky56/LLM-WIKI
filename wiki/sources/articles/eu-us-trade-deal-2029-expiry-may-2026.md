# EU-US Trade Deal Ratified: 2029 Expiry on Tariff Safeguards

## What Happened

The EU Council and Parliament formally ratified the EU-US trade agreement on **May 20, 2026**, striking a deal to implement the tariff elements of the Joint Statement. The deal includes a **2029 expiry date** on the tariff safeguard provisions, creating a defined sunset window that either party can activate.

## Why It Matters

The critical difference between previous coverage and this development: the earlier reporting captured the *political agreement in principle* (last week). This ratification converts that into a **legally structured bilateral framework** with a concrete review date. The 2029 expiry means both parties have three years to demonstrate the framework's value before automatic re-negotiation is triggered.

Context from carryover: this deal stabilizes what was previously a chaotic tariff environment — the Supreme Court's May 2026 ruling invalidated unilateral tariff authority, removing the legal basis for open-ended US tariff escalation. The EU ratified this deal knowing that the US no longer has the legal instrument to weaponize tariffs in the same way. The 2029 expiry effectively locks in that stability while creating a structured re-engagement point.
