# Magnifica Humanitas — Wikipedia

*Magnifica Humanitas* is the first encyclical explicitly addressing artificial intelligence, released by Pope Leo XIV on May 25, 2026.

## Key Themes

- **Human dignity in AI era** — AI development must center human flourishing
- **International AI governance** — calls for cooperative frameworks among nations
- **Vulnerable populations** — protection from AI harms
- **Technological accountability** — developers responsible for societal impact

## Context

Pope Leo XIV (born Robert Francis Prevost) issued the encyclical as a call to action for global AI governance. The document has been endorsed or referenced by:
- Anthropic (via Chris Olah's public remarks)
- Microsoft (through Copilot integrations and Vatican engagement)
- Google DeepMind
