# Microsoft AI Blog — May 2026 Roundup

Microsoft's AI blog features five case studies demonstrating AI deployment in diverse real-world settings.

## Key Articles

### Why better AI starts with the people it often misses
Microsoft's AI equity initiative focuses on prosthetic arm users and accessibility technology. Features people with disabilities as co-designers of AI systems.

### At aged care provider Regis, AI takes on paperwork
Regis (Australian aged care) deploys Microsoft Copilot for administrative tasks, freeing staff to focus on resident care. Demonstrates AI as workforce augmentation in care sectors.

### Costa Rican dairy cooperative turns AI agents into coworkers
Dos Pinos dairy cooperative uses Microsoft AI agents for production monitoring and quality control. AI operates as team member rather than tool.

### 4 ways AI is enabling the future of industrial work
Manufacturing AI deployment covers predictive maintenance, quality inspection, production optimization, and worker safety. Features Arum (manufacturing AI company).

### Porsche Cup Brasil: AI-powered crash analysis
Microsoft AI analyzes racing car crash data for safety improvement. AI accelerates post-incident analysis for motorsport engineering.
