# Russian Drone Hits Romanian Apartment — First Attack on NATO Territory (2026-05-29)

## What Happened
A Russian drone targeting Ukraine crashed into a **residential apartment building in Romania** (Galați region), injuring at least 2 people. NATO and EU both condemned Russia. Romania is considering invoking **NATO Article 4** (consultation when a member's territorial integrity is threatened).

This is the **first confirmed Russian attack on NATO territory** since the invasion began.

## Significance
- **NATO escalation threshold crossed**: Until now, Russian attacks near NATO borders have been characterized as accidents or spillover. This is intentional target drift into a NATO member — a qualitatively different event
- **Article 4 consultations**: If invoked, triggers mandatory consultation among all NATO members. Not the same as Article 5 (collective defense), but represents a significant escalation in alliance consultation
- **Ukraine war expanding geographic scope**: Romania is on NATO's eastern flank. Drone reaching Galați (western Romania, ~30km from Ukrainian border) suggests either navigation errors or deliberate probing
- **Al Jazeera live blog active**: "Iran war live: Trump due to make 'final determination'" is also trending — multiple simultaneous crises
- **Nato/EU condemnation**: Unified Western response signals this will be treated seriously

## Broader Context
This occurs as:
- Trump is deciding on Iran deal (ceasefire extension)
- Multiple NATO countries are increasing defense spending
- Ukraine-Russia negotiations continue in parallel
