# Pope Leo XIV — "Magnifica humanitas": The First AI Encyclical

## Summary

Pope Leo XIV released his first major theological document — the encyclical *Magnifica humanitas* — on **May 26, 2026**, making it the first papal encyclical to address artificial intelligence as a primary subject. The document frames AI development as an **arms-control and security problem** requiring binding international treaties, using the language of "disarmament" rather than regulation or ethics. The encyclical calls for AI to "serve humanity, not concentrate power."

## Key Content

- **Core thesis:** AI must be "disarmed" — explicitly using security/disarmament framing
- AI development framed as arms race requiring treaty-style controls, not voluntary guidelines
- **Binding rules on AI companies** demanded, not self-regulation or soft-law frameworks
- Power concentration identified as the primary theological danger of AI
- AI's threat characterized as analogous to weapons of mass destruction in its potential for concentrated, uncontrolled harm
- Draws parallel to the Industrial Revolution — suggesting AI's societal disruption is similarly irreversible without coordinated governance

## Vatican Diplomatic Follow-Through

The Vatican's diplomatic machinery around encyclicals typically operates on these tracks:

1. **Bilateral diplomatic outreach** — Vatican's 183-country diplomatic network delivers copies to heads of state and foreign ministers; official commentary often follows at UN and regional bodies
2. **UN engagement** — Vatican has permanent observer status at the UN; encyclicals on security topics frequently become discussion points in General Assembly and specialized agencies (ITU, UNESCO)
3. **Synod/Bishops' conferences** — Catholic bishops' conferences worldwide receive encyclicals for implementation guidance; creates layered pressure on governments through church networks in each country
4. **Follow-up events** — The Vatican often hosts conferences or working groups to push encyclical themes; expect a dedicated AI governance conference in 3-6 months

**As of May 28 (2 days post-publication):** Formal Vatican diplomatic follow-through not yet documented in available sources. The press conference and initial media campaign represent phase 1. Phase 2 (bilateral outreach, UN discussion) typically begins 1-2 weeks post-publication.

## Context

This is a qualitatively new voice in global AI governance. The Pope has no direct regulatory authority, but:
- The Catholic Church has 1.4 billion adherents — the world's largest religious institution
- The Vatican's diplomatic network spans 183 countries with formal observer status at the UN
- Papal encyclicals carry theological authority for 1+ billion Catholics and significant cultural weight beyond the faithful

With the US federal AI regulatory path definitively closed (Trump abandoned AI model vetting May 22), and California functioning as *de facto* US regulator, the Vatican's intervention adds a global governance pressure point that the US tech industry cannot simply dismiss. The arms-control framing is significant because it makes AI development a security treaty problem rather than a trade/industrial policy problem — changing the coalition of actors who need to be persuaded.
