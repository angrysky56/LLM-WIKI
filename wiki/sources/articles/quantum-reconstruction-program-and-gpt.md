# Quantum Reconstruction Program and GPT

A Gemini AI conversation (10 messages) exploring the **Quantum Reconstruction Program** — a research program that seeks to derive quantum theory from first principles rather than treating it as axiomatic.

## Key Topics

### Principle vs. Constructive Theories
The conversation distinguishes between **Principle Theories** (like thermodynamics and special relativity, which start from empirical postulates) and **Constructive Theories** (which build models from simpler components). Quantum Reconstruction follows the principle theory approach: "we lack the empirical facts to set as axioms."

### Generalized Probability Theory (GPT)
**GPT** is used as a mathematical framework for expressing the operational axioms of physical theories. The conversation examines how quantum theory fits within the GPT formalism.

### Continuous Reversibility
A key constraint explored is **Continuous Reversibility** — the requirement that any pure state must be smoothly and continuously transformable into any other pure state without destroying information.

### Capacity Limit
Discusses the **Capacity Limit** of quantum systems as a foundational constraint.
