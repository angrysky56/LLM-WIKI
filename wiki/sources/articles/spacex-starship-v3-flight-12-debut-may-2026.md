# SpaceX Starship V3 Completes Debut Test Flight Ahead of June 12 IPO

**Flight 12: Starship V3 (scaled-up version) achieves mostly successful debut**

SpaceX successfully launched its first Starship Version 3 — the tallest and most powerful rocket ever built — on May 23, 2026. The debut test flight of the scaled-up Starship ended in a fiery splashdown in the Indian Ocean as planned, a milestone for the vehicle. The successful launch directly supports SpaceX's $1.5 trillion Forbes valuation and the June 12 Nasdaq listing.

## Key Developments

### Starship V3 Debut (Flight 12)
- **First launch attempt scrubbed** May 21 — multiple last-minute issues, retry May 22
- **Successful launch May 23** from SpaceX Starbase, Texas
- Starship V3 is the most scaled-up, "beefed-up" version to date
- Ship achieved planned splashdown in Indian Ocean after completing primary mission objectives
- Fortune reported an early SpaceX investor said the next-gen Starship enables the high valuation — direct connection between V3 success and IPO confidence

### Starship V3 Significance for IPO
- SpaceX targeting **June 12 Nasdaq listing** (Nasdaq ticker expected)
- Forbes valuation: **$1.5 trillion**
- V3 is the operational version expected to support Starlink Gen 3 and future crewed missions
- Successful V3 test reduces IPO risk — investors buying into a vehicle program with demonstrated capability

### Context
- Flight 12 was the 12th Starship test flight overall
- Previous V3 test campaign (Flight 11) was successful before V3 debut
- The IPO is closely watched as the largest private company listing in history
