# Trump Defers Iran Deal — 'Final Determination' Pending (2026-05-29)

## What Happened
Trump held a Situation Room meeting May 29 to make a "final determination" on the US-Iran framework deal, but **no announcement was made**. Multiple sources confirm US and Iranian negotiators have reached a tentative deal to extend the ceasefire and begin new nuclear talks. Trump called it "very productive" but said he needs more time.

Reuters: Trump demands reopening of Hormuz Strait as part of any deal.

## Key Details
- **US-Iran deal reached**: Sources to Axios, AP confirm a tentative framework exists
- **Trump's ask**: Reopening of Hormuz Strait (strategic chokepoint for global oil)
- **Iran hardliners trying to derail**: Hardliners in Tehran are actively working against the deal
- **Kazakhstan offer**: Kazakhstan offered to take Iran's uranium stockpile (IAEA watchdog confirmed) — a confidence-building measure
- **No announcement**: Trump left the meeting without signing off. Final determination still pending as of May 29

## Why It Matters
- This would be the most significant US-Iran diplomatic breakthrough in decades
- Hormuz Strait reopening would reshape global oil markets and Gulf geopolitics
- Ceasefire extension would affect ongoing tensions across the Middle East
- Timing: concurrent with Russia/NATO crisis, SpaceX IPO, Ebola outbreak — multiple simultaneous global flashpoints
