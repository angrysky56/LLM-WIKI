# Tulsi Gabbard Resigns as US National Intelligence Director

## What Happened

Tulsi Gabbard has announced her resignation as US National Intelligence Director, citing her husband's illness as the reason. Gabbard had been largely out of public view during recent US military operations in the Middle East. Her departure leaves a key intelligence leadership position vacant at a time when the US is managing multiple global crises including the Iran conflict, Russia-Ukraine war, and ongoing tensions in the South China Sea.

## Why It Matters

- **Intelligence community leadership vacuum**: The National Intelligence Director oversees all 18 US intelligence agencies. A vacancy during active conflict periods creates coordination risks
- **Precedent of resignations under Trump administration**: Gabbard's resignation adds to a pattern of early departures from key national security posts
- **Successor unknown**: No successor has been named; the Deputy Director typically handles continuity, but strategic decisions require Senate-confirmed leadership
