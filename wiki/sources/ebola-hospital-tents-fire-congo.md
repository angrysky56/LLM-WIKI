# Angry Crowd Sets Ebola Hospital Tents on Fire in DR Congo

## What Happened

Grieving relatives and friends of a suspected Ebola victim attempted to retrieve the body of their deceased loved one from Rwampara General Hospital in the Democratic Republic of Congo for a traditional burial. When hospital officials and security personnel prevented them, the crowd set fire to the Ebola treatment center tents. Police were present and physically positioned in front of the makeshift structures. The incident underscores the breakdown in community trust and public health infrastructure in the ongoing Ebola outbreak.

## Why It Matters

1. **Response infrastructure collapsing**: This is the second major incident indicating the Ebola response is failing at a community level. Hospital attacks and resistance to safe burial protocols suggest the outbreak may be significantly under-reported if communities are actively avoiding or attacking treatment centers.

2. **Transmission risk**: Open flames near a facility handling highly infectious biological material creates cross-contamination hazards. The fire may have destroyed protective equipment and isolation infrastructure.

3. **WHO emergency declaration at risk**: With 600+ suspected cases and 139+ deaths as of May 20, the outbreak is already at a scale that warrants WHO emergency committee review. Community violence against treatment centers will likely push the declaration decision sooner.
