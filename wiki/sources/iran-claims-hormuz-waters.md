# Iran Publishes Map Claiming Control Over 22,000 sq km of Strait of Hormuz

## What Happened

Iran's armed forces published a map claiming formal "oversight" across more than 22,000 square kilometers of the Strait of Hormuz — the narrow waterway through which roughly 20% of global oil traded passes. The map represents a direct challenge to freedom of navigation principles and escalates ongoing tensions during what has been a fragile and repeatedly violated ceasefire with the United States.

## Why It Matters

1. **Ceasefire stress test**: The Hormuz claim coincides with Qatar-mediated indirect US-Iran talks. Iran may be attempting to establish new facts on the ground before negotiations conclude, or it may be a negotiating tactic to improve leverage. Either way, it tests the ceasefire's durability.

2. **Global oil transit at stake**: The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most important oil chokepoint. Any claim of Iranian control over 22,000+ sq km — even if not enforced — adds risk premium to global energy markets and creates flashpoint potential with US Navy presence in the Gulf.

3. **Ceasefire status unclear**: BBC Verify is carrying this as a breaking story today, meaning the ceasefire that was reportedly holding is now under direct challenge from the Iranian side with an explicit territorial claim.
