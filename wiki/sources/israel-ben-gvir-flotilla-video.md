# How Ben-Gvir's Flotilla Video Shattered Israel's Multimillion Hasbara

## What Happened

Israeli far-right National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir filmed himself taunting Gaza aid flotilla activists who had been abducted by Israeli forces. The video showed activists kneeling with their hands tied behind their backs while Ben-Gvir mocked them — footage that went viral across global media. The activists were part of a civilian aid flotilla attempting to breach Israel's naval blockade of Gaza. Israel deported the activists after the backlash intensified, and Ben-Gvir was forced into damage control.

## Why It Matters

The incident is significant for three reasons:

1. **Hasbara collapse**: Israel spends millions annually on public diplomacy (hasbara) to shape global narratives around Gaza and the conflict. Ben-Gvir's taunting video undid significant work in a single clip — one that spread faster than any official Israeli response could contain.

2. **Accountability gap**: The incident exposes how internal political incentives (Ben-Gvir playing to his base) directly undermine strategic diplomatic objectives. This is a systemic problem, not an isolated one.

3. **Global signal**: The incident joins the Air France manslaughter verdict and Francesca Albanese sanctions as part of a pattern where Israeli officials face fewer accountability constraints than in previous cycles — increasing the risk of further incidents.
