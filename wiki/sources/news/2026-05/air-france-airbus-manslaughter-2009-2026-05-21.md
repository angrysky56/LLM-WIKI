# Air France and Airbus Found Guilty of Manslaughter — 2009 Flight AF447 Crash

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** BBC News  
**Original URL:** https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/czd2qmdvmq6o

## Summary

Paris Appeals Court convicted Air France and Airbus of manslaughter over the crash of Air France Flight AF447 (Rio-Paris) on June 1, 2009, which killed all 228 people aboard. The landmark ruling is the first-ever conviction of a company for manslaughter in France, marking a watershed for corporate accountability in aviation. The crash was caused by ice crystals clogging pitot tubes, leading to aerodynamic stall at cruise altitude over the Atlantic.

## Key Facts

- **Convicted parties:** Air France (airline) and Airbus (aircraft manufacturer)
- **Verdict:** Manslaughter — company executives were previously acquitted but the companies were found criminally liable
- **Sentence:** Fines and damages to be determined in civil proceedings
- **Significance:** First time a French court has convicted a corporation for manslaughter; precedent for aviation industry accountability worldwide
