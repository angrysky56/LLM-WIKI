# Asteroid 2026 JH2 to Narrowly Miss Earth

## Summary
Astronomers discovered a new near-Earth asteroid, 2026 JH2, just days before it is expected to fly past Earth at a close range — closer than the Moon's orbit. The object is roughly the size of a blue whale. It ranks zero on the Torino Scale (no hazard). Live tracking available online.

## Key Facts
- **Asteroid 2026 JH2** discovered only days earlier
- Size comparable to a blue whale
- Flyby distance closer than the Moon
- Ranked **zero on the Torino Scale** — no significant hazard
- Live online tracking available (Space.com)
