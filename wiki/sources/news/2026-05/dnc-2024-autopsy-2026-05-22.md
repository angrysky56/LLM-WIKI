# DNC 2024 Election Autopsy Report — 2026-05

## What Happened

The Democratic National Committee released its formal 2024 election autopsy report in May 2026 — an 80+ page post-mortem on the Harris loss. DNC Chair Jamie Harrison apologized for the process having "created an even bigger distraction." Key findings:

- **Economic contrast failure**: Campaign did not effectively contrast Trump's economic record with Harris's
- **Biden age questions unaddressed**: Questions about Biden's fitness were not addressed early enough in the cycle
- **Coalition turnout collapse**: Black, Latino, and young voters did not turn out at required levels
- **Progressive policy contrast absent**: Campaign failed to make the case for progressive policies against Trump

The autopsy itself became politically contentious — multiple party figures publicly rejected the findings, with internal disputes about blame attribution.

## Why It Matters

The autopsy is both a diagnostic document and a political weapon — internal factions use it to argue for different futures. The fact that the report's release generated its own controversy suggests the party remains in a pre-synthesis fracture state. This has implications for:
- 2028 primary positioning
- Platform content and contrast strategy
- Coalition voter outreach methodology
- The institutional-capture vs. populist framing debate
