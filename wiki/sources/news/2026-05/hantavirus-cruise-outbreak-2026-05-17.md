# Hantavirus Cruise Ship Outbreak Spreads to North America

## Summary
A hantavirus outbreak originating on a cruise ship has spread to North America, with Canada's national health agency confirming the first positive test in a patient in British Columbia. The ship docked in the Netherlands for disinfection. The outbreak is prompting comparisons to early Covid-era responses and some conspiracy theories.

## Key Facts
- **Hantavirus-stricken cruise ship** docked in Netherlands for disinfection
- **First North American case** confirmed in Canadian patient in B.C.
- Canadian national health agency confirmed 1 positive hantavirus test (AP)
- Outbreak prompting "Covid flashbacks" — including conspiracy theories
- Rat virus origin; close sustained contact required for transmission
