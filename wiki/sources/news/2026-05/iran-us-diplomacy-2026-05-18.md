# Iran Revised Proposal / US Diplomatic Row

## Summary
Iran submitted a revised proposal to end the ongoing war to the United States through a Pakistani intermediary on May 18, 2026. Iranian state media confirmed the proposal. Meanwhile, Trump publicly warned Iran that "the clock is ticking" and new drone attacks struck the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Tehran acknowledged submitting a new proposal.

## Key Facts
- **Iran submitted revised proposal** to end the war to US via Pakistani source (Reuters, May 18)
- **Drone attacks** hit UAE and Saudi Arabia; Iran-linked strikes reported
- **Trump** warned "TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE" for Iran deal (ABC News)
- Tehran confirmed the new proposal; scale and terms not yet public
