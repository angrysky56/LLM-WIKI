# JWST Observes Ancient Galaxies That Challenge Models

## Summary
The James Webb Space Telescope is currently observing galaxies that formed approximately 13 billion years ago, several of which should not exist according to the Lambda-CDM model cosmologists were using when Webb was launched. The findings continue to challenge established theories of early galaxy formation.

## Key Facts
- JWST observing galaxies from ~13 billion years ago
- Several galaxies **should not exist** per pre-launch Lambda-CDM model
- Astronomers continue to analyze and reinterpret findings
- Additional puzzle: supernova dust may explain some anomalous observations
- Brown dwarfs may be masquerading as early galaxies in some observations
