# NASA Psyche Mars Flyby — 2026-05-21

## What Happened

NASA's Psyche spacecraft successfully completed a Mars flyby on May 21, 2026, capturing spectacular and previously unavailable views of the red planet as it swung past for a gravitational assist. The spacecraft is en route to asteroid 16 Psyche, a metal-rich world in the main asteroid belt that may be the exposed core of a protoplanet.

## Why It Matters

16 Psyche is one of the largest known M-type (metal-rich) asteroids in our solar system. Scientists believe it may be the remnant of a planetary core that was stripped of its outer rocky mantle billions of years ago, possibly during violent collisions. Visiting it directly offers a unique window into:
- How terrestrial planets like Earth formed internal layered structures
- The mechanics of core differentiation in the early solar system
- The potential for metal asteroid mining in the future

The mission is humanity's first visit to a metal-rich world. Arrival at 16 Psyche is targeted for 2029.
