# Massive Natural Hydrogen Deposit Discovered Beneath Canada

## What Happened

Scientists announced the discovery of a major natural hydrogen deposit underground in Canada. Natural hydrogen, also called "white hydrogen," is generated through geological processes including water-rock reactions and radioactive decay. Unlike green hydrogen (produced by electrolysis using renewable energy) or blue hydrogen (from natural gas with carbon capture), natural hydrogen requires no manufacturing process, making it potentially the most cost-effective and low-emission source of hydrogen.

## Why It Matters

If the deposit proves economically viable, it could represent a significant shift in the global energy landscape. Natural hydrogen could serve as a carbon-free fuel for:
- Heavy industry and steel production
- Transportation (particularly maritime and aviation)
- Power generation

This discovery also validates geological hydrogen as a real and potentially abundant resource, encouraging further exploration globally. The implications for AI research are indirect but notable: energy availability and cost affect data center operations and model training economics.

## Key Facts

- **Location**: Underground Canada
- **Resource**: Natural (white) hydrogen
- **Formation**: Water-rock reactions, radioactive decay
- **Advantage**: No manufacturing process required; potentially lowest-cost clean hydrogen
