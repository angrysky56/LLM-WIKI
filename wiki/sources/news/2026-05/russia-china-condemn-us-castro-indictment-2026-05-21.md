# Russia and China Condemn US Over Raúl Castro Indictment

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** BBC News  
**Original URL:** https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/clype0x7pkgo

## Summary

Beijing and Moscow rally behind Havana after the US indicted former Cuban leader Raúl Castro for the 1996 downing of two dissident planes that killed four Cuban-American exiles. The move escalates great-power tensions at the same time as the Xi-Putin summit, with China and Russia jointly condemning what they frame as American imperial overreach.

## Key Facts

- **Indicted:** Raúl Castro (former Cuban leader, brother of Fidel)
- **Charge:** Involved in 1996 shootdown of two Brothers to the Rescue planes — killed 4 Cuban-American exiles
- **Great-power response:** China and Russia both condemned the indictment as American overreach
- **Timing:** Same day as Xi-Putin summit — signals coordinated messaging
- **Context:** Part of broader US pressure campaign against Cuba
