# Samsung Workers Prepare for Major Strike: ~50,000 Employees

## What Happened

Approximately 50,000 Samsung workers in South Korea are preparing to strike, representing one of the largest labor actions in the country's corporate history. The strike stems from ongoing tensions between Samsung management and workers regarding wages, working conditions, and benefits. Samsung, the world's largest manufacturer of memory chips and a critical supplier to AI hardware chains, could face significant production disruptions if the strike proceeds.

## Why It Matters

Samsung is the dominant global supplier of HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) chips used in AI accelerators like NVIDIA's GPUs. Any significant disruption to Samsung's chip production would ripple through the global AI hardware supply chain, potentially affecting:
- AI data center expansion timelines
- GPU availability and pricing
- Consumer electronics production

The timing is particularly significant given the ongoing AI chip shortage and the strategic importance of semiconductor manufacturing to national economies.

- **Risk**: Major disruption to global chip supply chain
