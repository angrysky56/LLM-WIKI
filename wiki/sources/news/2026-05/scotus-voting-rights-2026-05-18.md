# SCOTUS Voting Rights Ruling Ripples Through Local Level

## Summary
A Supreme Court ruling on voting rights is producing swift action at the local level, particularly in Louisiana and Alabama. Black Democrats are warning that minority-majority legislative districts are at risk, with some accusing Republicans of creating a "Jim Crow 2.0" through redistricting. The ruling complicates Black voters' sense of political place in the South.

## Key Facts
- Supreme Court voting rights decision spurred swift action in Louisiana and Alabama
- Black Democrats warn **minority-majority districts at risk**
- "Jim Crow 2.0" accusations leveled at redistricting efforts
- NPR analysis: ruling could play "big role" at local level
- Southern Democrats describe the situation as "the entire South is on fire"
