# Gonorrhoea and Syphilis Hit Record Levels in Europe

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** BBC News  
**Original URL:** https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cm2pj07dr7lo

## Summary

European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) data reveals record levels of sexually transmitted infections across Europe. Gonorrhoea cases surged 303% since 2015 to 106,331 annual cases, while syphilis cases more than doubled to 45,557. Congenital syphilis cases nearly doubled, indicating widespread undetected infection in pregnant populations.

## Key Facts

- **Gonorrhoea:** 106,331 cases (303% increase since 2015) — bacterial, increasingly antibiotic-resistant
- **Syphilis:** 45,557 cases (more than doubled since 2015) — bacterial, treatable but can cause serious organ damage
- **Congenital syphilis:** Nearly doubled — infections passed to newborns, often fatal or debilitating
- **Root causes:** Reduced condom use, less STI testing post-COVID, antibiotic resistance
- **Public health impact:** Straining European healthcare systems
