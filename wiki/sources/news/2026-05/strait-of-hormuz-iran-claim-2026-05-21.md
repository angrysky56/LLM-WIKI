# Iran Expands Strait of Hormuz Claim — New Military Authority Created

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** BBC News  
**Original URL:** https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cq5py64gvwzo

## Summary

Iran announced the creation of a "Persian Gulf Strait Authority" claiming military oversight of over 22,000 square kilometers — including Omani and UAE territorial waters — in a direct challenge to US naval dominance of the critical oil shipping lane. The move escalates already-high tensions over the Strait of Hormuz, through which roughly 20-25% of global oil trade passes.

## Key Facts

- **Claimed area:** 22,000+ sq km of waterway, including portions of Oman and UAE territorial seas
- **Claimed authority:** Iranian military oversight body for the "Persian Gulf Strait Authority"
- **Strategic context:** Strait of Hormuz is the world's most critical oil chokepoint — 20-25% of global oil trade transits through it
- **Challenge to:** US Fifth Fleet operations and longstanding international maritime law
