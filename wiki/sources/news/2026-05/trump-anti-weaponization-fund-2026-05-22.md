# Trump's $1.8B Anti-Weaponization Fund — GOP Revolt, DOJ Memo Reveals Slush Fund — 2026-05-21

## What Happened

A DOJ memo to Republican senators, released publicly May 21, 2026, detailed how Trump's $1.8 billion 'anti-weaponization' fund would function as a mechanism to funnel money to Trump allies outside normal congressional oversight. The disclosure triggered immediate Republican revolt in the Senate — a scheduled budget vote was delayed and GOP senators called off votes on the fund.

Trump's allies have already begun lining up to apply for the fund. January 6 officers have filed a lawsuit attempting to stop the fund.
