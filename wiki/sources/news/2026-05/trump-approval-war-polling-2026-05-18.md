# Trump Approval Sinks Amid Iran War

## Summary
A New York Times poll published May 18, 2026 shows President Trump's approval rating declining sharply amid an unpopular Iran war. The polling darkens GOP prospects ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. Support for the war and for Israel also showed significant decline among US voters.

## Key Facts
- **Trump approval sinks** amid unpopular Iran war (NYT, May 18)
- War support and Israel support both waned in polling
- GOP prospects described as "darkening" by NYT
- Poll conducted by NYT in partnership with Siena College
