# Trump Oscillates Between Diplomacy and Military Threats in Iran Standoff

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** Al Jazeera  
**Original URL:** https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/21/trump-shifts-between-diplomacy-and-threats-in-iran-standoff

## Summary

The US-Iran war remains in a fragile ceasefire as President Trump alternates between threatening to resume military strikes and expressing hope for a negotiated settlement. Iran has submitted a 14-point peace plan through a Pakistani intermediary, while simultaneously escalating its claim over the Strait of Hormuz. Drone attacks have continued against US allies UAE and Saudi Arabia, keeping the situation volatile.

## Key Facts

- **Ceasefire status:** Fragile — US-Iran hostilities paused but not formally ended
- **Iran peace proposal:** 14-point plan submitted via Pakistani intermediary
- **Trump posture:** Alternating between military threats and diplomatic overtures
- **Ongoing attacks:** Drone strikes hit UAE and Saudi Arabia — attribution unclear but Iran-linked
- **Key flashpoint:** Strait of Hormuz — Iran creating new maritime authority claiming control
