# Trump Targets Republican Critic Thomas Massie in Primary

## Summary
President Trump is actively working to unseat Representative Thomas Massie, one of his most consistent Republican critics, in the Kentucky primary. Trump's efforts follow Bill Cassidy's defeat in Louisiana. Massie has described himself as "unfazed" by the attacks. Trump called Massie's ouster effort "desperate."

## Key Facts
- **Trump attacking Massie** as Republican critic; calls effort to oust him "desperate"
- Bill Cassidy (R-LA) recently **defeated** in Louisiana primary
- Massie calls himself "unfazed" ahead of Kentucky primary
- Trump eyes Kentucky's Thomas Massie in "MAGA purge" (USA Today)
- Trump allies pursuing "desperate" attempts to remove Massie from primary ballot
