# Trump-Backed Prayer Rally Draws Thousands to National Mall

## Summary
A Trump-backed prayer festival drew thousands to the National Mall on May 18, 2026, coinciding with America's 250th birthday celebrations. Trump and administration officials addressed the Christian gathering. Organizers proclaimed "We welcome Jesus!" The event represented a significant fusion of political and religious messaging ahead of the 2026 midterms.

## Key Facts
- **Trump-backed prayer festival** on National Mall — thousands gathered
- Event tagline: "We welcome Jesus!" (Washington Post)
- Part of America's **250th birthday** celebration
- Trump and administration officials addressed the gathering
- Described as rededicate prayer event; religious rally on national mall
