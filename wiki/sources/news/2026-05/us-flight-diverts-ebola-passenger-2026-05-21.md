# US-Bound Flight Diverts After Passenger From Ebola Region Boards In Error

**Date:** 2026-05-21  
**Source:** BBC News  
**Original URL:** https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0m2k79mv2ko

## Summary

An Air France flight from Paris to Detroit was diverted to Montreal after a passenger from the Democratic Republic of Congo — a region under US entry restrictions due to the ongoing Ebola outbreak — was mistakenly allowed to board. The incident exposed gaps in screening protocols and marked the first visible travel restriction resulting from the WHO emergency declaration.

## Key Facts

- **Flight:** Air France, Paris (CDG) → Detroit, diverted to Montreal
- **Passenger origin:** DRC Ebola outbreak region — subject to US entry restrictions
- **Screening failure:** Passenger allowed to board in error
- **Outcome:** Flight diverted to Montreal; passenger removed
- **Significance:** First visible travel restriction from WHO emergency declaration
