## OpenAI Joins AI IPO Pipeline Worth $3.6 Trillion

OpenAI has joined a pipeline of high-profile AI and technology IPOs now valued at a combined $3.6 trillion, according to Bloomberg reporting. This follows the SpaceX IPO filing (priced at $75B valuation / $135 per share for June 12) and Anthropic's confidential SEC filing.

### Significance

- Represents the largest technology IPO pipeline in history
- Signals the market's willingness to absorb multiple mega-cap tech IPOs simultaneously
- Follows a period of AI stock volatility — the Nasdaq sell-off in early June had raised questions about IPO timing
- BNP Paribas now forecasting three Fed rate hikes starting December 2026, which could affect IPO pricing

### Key Details

- No specific IPO date or valuation for OpenAI disclosed yet
- Pipeline includes SpaceX, Anthropic, OpenAI, and other AI infrastructure companies
- Combined pipeline value of $3.6T exceeds GDP of most countries
