# Anthropic Confidentially Files for IPO (2026-06-02)

## Significance

Anthropic filed an SEC IPO prospectus confidentially (CNBC), with US share sale planned for later this year at a valuation approaching $1 trillion. The filing is the second trillion-dollar AI public-market event in a single quarter — SpaceX's IPO is scheduled for 2026-06-12. Together, these two listings mark 2026 as the year the AI economy crossed into public markets at trillion-dollar scale.

## Key facts

- Confidentially filed IPO prospectus with SEC (CNBC 2026-06-01 16:04 GMT)
- Plans US share sale later this year (BBC 2026-06-02 07:00 GMT)
- Already trading at ~$1T on secondary markets (Business Insider 2026-04-22)
- Anthropic has issued warnings on unauthorized secondary platforms (Axios 2026-05-13, TechCrunch 2026-05-12)

## Why it matters

- **AI bubble debate intensifies**: NYT (2026-05-29) "Sky-High I.P.O. Pricing Isn't Great for Real People" frames the public reception. Figma's post-IPO correction is the cautionary tale.
- **OpenAI competitive pressure**: Anthropic becoming a publicly-comparable company narrows OpenAI's fundraising leverage.
- **Cap-table expansion**: $1T AI listings in the same quarter is unprecedented — both for retail-accessible AI exposure and for the size of paper wealth being created.
