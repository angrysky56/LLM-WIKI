## Pashinyan Wins Re-election in Armenia

Incumbent Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan won re-election in Armenia on June 9, 2026, securing a third term amid intensifying Russian pressure on his pro-Western government. The election was widely seen as a referendum on Armenia's shift away from traditional Moscow alignment toward closer ties with the EU and NATO.

### Significance

- Armenia's geopolitical reorientation continues under Pashinyan
- Russia has been piling pressure on the pro-West government
- Election outcome affects Caucasus power dynamics
- Follows Armenia's suspension of CSTO participation and deepening EU integration

### Key Details

- Third term for Pashinyan
- Election came amid Russian pressure campaign
- Armenia has been pivoting toward the West since the 2020 Nagorno-Karabakh war
- Results being watched closely by EU, US, Russia, and Azerbaijan
