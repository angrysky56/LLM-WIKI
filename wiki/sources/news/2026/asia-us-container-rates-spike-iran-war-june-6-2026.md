# Asia-to-US container rates spike 109% since Iran war started

Container shipping rates from Asia to the United States have surged 109% since the Iran war began, Bloomberg reported on June 6, 2026. The spike reflects the profound impact of Middle Eastern conflict on global supply chains.

Key economic indicators:
• Asia-to-US container rates more than doubled, with further increases expected
• Shipping disruptions compound existing tariff pressures, including the Trump administration's new forced-labor tariffs on 60 economies
• The dollar's status as global reserve currency faces new scrutiny — Bloomberg's 'Is the Dollar Losing Its Luster?' explores the question
• Indonesia's markets face a crisis of confidence as investors decode President Prabowo's economic policies

The container rate spike represents one of the clearest transmission mechanisms from geopolitical conflict to consumer prices, with implications for inflation and central bank policy.
