## Anti-Immigrant Violence Erupts in Belfast After Knife Attack

A wave of anti-immigrant violence swept through **Belfast, Northern Ireland**, following a brutal stabbing attack. This represents the most significant outbreak of anti-immigrant unrest in the UK in recent years.

### Key Developments

- **Violence**: Rioters clashed with police and attacked immigrant-owned businesses (NYT, Washington Post)
- **Trigger**: A street stabbing in Belfast sparked the unrest (CBS News, NBC News)
- **Response**: UK leaders called for calm as protests spread (BBC)
- **Political context**: PM Starmer faces a potential destabilizing by-election in Makerfield (NYT)

### Broader European Context

The Belfast violence reflects broader European trends of rising anti-immigrant sentiment, exacerbated by economic pressures and housing shortages. Northern Ireland's post-Brexit political landscape has seen increased sectarian and racial tensions.

### Global Pattern

The unrest coincides with simultaneous anti-immigrant outbreaks:
- **South Africa**: Xenophobic attacks force 300 Ghanaians to leave; Ramaphosa vows crackdown (DW, HRW, The Conversation)
- **US**: Trump administration targeting immigrants from climate-affected countries (Guardian)
