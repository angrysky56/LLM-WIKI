# California Governor Newsom Signs AI Worker Protection Executive Order (May 2026)

## What Happened

California Governor **Gavin Newsom** signed a **first-of-its-kind executive order** on May 21, 2026, directing state agencies to prepare workers and businesses for potential AI disruption. This is the first explicit state-level policy attempt to proactively address AI-driven job displacement rather than react to it.

## Key Provisions

The order addresses:
- **Worker retraining pipelines** for AI-displaced workers
- **Business transition support** for companies adopting AI
- **Economic safety nets** for communities facing AI-driven job losses
- **State workforce planning** to anticipate AI impact by sector

## Context

- Follows California becoming first state to regulate AI in 2024
- Comes as **Brazil's 2026 elections** are testing AI regulation in democratic contexts
- Context: Trump administration rolled back federal AI oversight — states picking up the slack
- States broadly plowing ahead with AI regulation despite White House resistance (NYT, March 2026)
