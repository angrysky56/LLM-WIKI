# Kenya Court Blocks US Ebola Quarantine Plan; 2 Killed in Protests (2026-06-02)

## Significance

The Kenya High Court ordered the government to release details of a planned US-Ebola-quarantine-center. Two people were shot dead in protests against the facility. This is a sovereignty test case: the US wants to use a Kenyan facility to quarantine American Ebola patients (bypassing the typical "evacuate to US/Europe" pattern), and the optics are fueling a sovereignty-and-racial-justice backlash.

## Key facts

- 2 shot dead in protests (BBC 2026-06-02 10:53 GMT, Reuters 2026-06-02 06:42 GMT)
- High Court orders details released (Al Jazeera 2026-06-02 09:20 GMT)
- "New Setback From Court Ruling" (NYT 2026-06-02 12:20 GMT)
- Court had previously blocked the plan (2026-05-29); ruling extended
- DRC outbreak continues: WHO calling for community cooperation; 3 vaccines in development (BBC)

## Why it matters

- **Sovereignty precedent**: If Kenya backs down, the precedent is set for US-dictated African health infrastructure. If it holds, African-loss-of-sovereignty narrative gains ground.
- **Public-health geopolitics**: The US treating its citizens in Africa near the outbreak zone is the trigger.
- **Escalation risk**: Two people shot dead in protests is the first significant anti-US-health-infrastructure violence in Kenya.
