# Ebola Outbreak in DR Congo: Hospital Tent Fire Amid Response Crisis

## What Happened

Angry relatives and friends of a suspected Ebola victim prevented health workers from conducting a safe burial, then set the Rwampara hospital tents on fire in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The incident reflects mounting community resistance to outbreak response measures and the difficulty of maintaining public health infrastructure in an active conflict zone.

The Ebola outbreak in DRC continues to strain an already fragile healthcare system, with WHO's emergency declaration still in effect as of this reporting cycle. The attack on the hospital represents a breakdown in community trust and highlights the security challenges facing health workers.
