## Core Insight

The Air France diversion is the first visible proof that US Ebola travel restrictions are being actively enforced at the border. The passenger boarded in error — CBP had to force the diversion mid-flight — showing the system caught a gap, not that the gap was exploited. One American doctor has already been infected working with a missionary group in DR Congo and is being treated in Germany.
