# EU-US Trade Deal: Council and Parliament Strike Agreement on Tariff Elements

**EU-US Joint Statement tariff implementation deal reached**

The European Council and Parliament reached a deal to implement the tariff elements of the EU-US Joint Statement, as reported May 20, 2026 by the Council of the EU. This represents progress in the EU-US trade relationship after the tariff conflicts of early 2026.

## Key Details

- The deal covers the **tariff elements of the Joint Statement** agreed between EU and US leadership
- The deal was struck between the Council and Parliament — a positive signal for rapid implementation
- This follows the period of heightened tariff tensions between US and EU, including Trump's 25% tariff threat on EU cars (May 1)
- Implementation timeline and specific tariff rate adjustments are specified in the deal

## Context

This deal comes amid an ongoing period of US-EU trade negotiation following:
- Trump's 25% tariff announcement on EU cars (May 1)
- US-China 90-day tariff truce extension (May 12)
- A US trade court ruling against Trump's 10% global tariff (May 7)
- EU retaliatory tariffs still in place on US goods despite the joint statement framework

The deal signals that EU-US trade relations are stabilizing on the tariff front, though the underlying issues (US steel/aluminum tariffs, aircraft subsidies) remain partially unresolved.
