# EU-US Trade Deal: Tariff Elements of Joint Statement Agreed

## What Happened

The EU Council and Parliament struck a deal on May 20, 2026 to implement the tariff elements of the EU-US Joint Statement. This represents a rapprochement in transatlantic trade relations after the tariff turbulence of 2025-2026.

The deal comes as:
- US trade court ruled Trump's 10% global tariff illegal (May 7)
- US-China trade talks ongoing with uncertainty
- Global tariff regime in flux with legal challenges

## Why It Matters

1. **Transatlantic stability**: The deal provides a stable framework for EU-US trade, reducing uncertainty for businesses on both sides
2. **Counterbalance to China**: EU-US deal isolates China in trade dynamics — China warned nations against "appeasing" US in trade deals
3. **EU positioning**: Deal demonstrates EU can negotiate bilaterally even as the US operates outside WTO frameworks
4. **Tariff architecture**: The deal may serve as template for US bilateral deals with other partners — Japan deal earlier showed the pattern
