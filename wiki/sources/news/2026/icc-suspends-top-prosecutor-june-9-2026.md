## ICC Suspends Top Prosecutor

The International Criminal Court has suspended its top prosecutor following an investigation into misconduct allegations. The suspension represents a significant institutional crisis for the court, which has been pursuing cases related to the Ukraine war, Gaza conflict, and other major international crimes.

### Significance

- Institutional crisis at a critical moment for international justice
- ICC has been pursuing high-profile cases related to multiple ongoing conflicts
- Timing raises questions about the court's ability to function during active war crimes investigations
- Could affect ongoing cases including Ukraine, Lebanon, and Sudan

### Key Details

- Suspension follows internal investigation into misconduct
- Specific allegations have not been fully disclosed
- Deputy prosecutor or acting leadership expected temporarily
- Court operations continue but cases may face delays

### Open Questions

- Length of suspension and outcome of investigation
- Impact on ongoing war crimes investigations
- Will this affect ICC credibility with member states?
