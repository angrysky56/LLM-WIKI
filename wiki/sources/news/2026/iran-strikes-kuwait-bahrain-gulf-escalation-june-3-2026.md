# Iran Strikes Kuwait and Bahrain — Gulf War Escalates (2026-06-03)

## What Happened

Iran launched a **missile and drone barrage** targeting **Kuwait and Bahrain** on 2026-06-03, striking civilian infrastructure including Kuwait International Airport. Kuwait confirmed **at least one killed, dozens injured**. Bahrain was also hit (Al Jazeera video feed; NYT live blog).

The US and Iran then **accused each other** of launching the new strikes (BBC). The exchange pushed the conflict onto a fundamentally different trajectory from the prior week's "Trump expects deal in a week" narrative.

## Why It Matters

- **Direct US-Iran exchange**: Both sides publicly claim the other struck first. The strikes are not proxy — both Kuwait and Bahrain host US military bases.
- **Rubio contradiction**: Secretary of State Rubio told US lawmakers **the war on Iran is "over"** — even as Iran was hitting Gulf allies the same day. Either the diplomacy is far more advanced than public, or there is a serious US government messaging fracture.
- **Trump-Khamenei meeting floated**: Trump told the New York Post Iran's supreme leader is involved in peace talks and he hopes to meet him — but the same day, Iran is striking US-aligned Gulf states.
- **Oil markets**: Bloomberg reported oil **extended gains for a third day** on the flare-up; US stock futures fell on the "peace hopes" reversal. Strait of Hormuz reopening narrative evaporating.
