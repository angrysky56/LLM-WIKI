# Israel & Lebanon Agree Conditional Ceasefire — Hezbollah Must Evacuate South of Litani

**Date**: 2026-06-03 announced / 2026-06-04 (publication)
**Significance**: 🟠 **HIGH** — First formal trilateral ceasefire framework since the April 16 deal collapsed. Adds concrete Hezbollah-disarmament condition to the "stop all shooting" partial ceasefire Trump announced 2026-06-02. Continues to be undermined by same-day strikes.

## What Happened

After a **fourth round of US-mediated trilateral talks in Washington**, Israel and Lebanon announced Wednesday (2026-06-03) a renewed ceasefire framework that:

1. **Requires "complete cessation" of Hezbollah attacks** as a condition.
2. **Establishes "pilot security zones" inside Lebanon** where only the Lebanese Armed Forces (LAF) operate — Hezbollah operatives would be "excluded" from these zones.
3. **Requires evacuation of all Hezbollah operatives** from the area between the Israeli border and the **Litani River (~30km / 19 miles north)** — currently occupied by Israeli ground forces.
4. **No maps provided** for the pilot zones; next talks scheduled for **22 June 2026** to "reach a comprehensive agreement."

The three parties — US, Lebanon, Israel — also "rejected any attempt, by any state or non-state actor, to hold Lebanon's future hostage."

## Conditionality Is the Story

This is materially different from the **2026-06-02 partial ceasefire** (Trump-announced "stop all shooting" deal):

| Dimension | 2026-06-02 Partial | 2026-06-04 Trilateral |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Beirut-only bombing moratorium | Border-to-Litani zone |
| Hezbollah condition | Implicit | Explicit: complete cessation + evacuation |
| Security architecture | Status quo | New LAF-only "pilot zones" |
| Negotiating venue | Trump statement | US State Department joint statement |
| Domestic opposition | — | Israeli far-right (Smotrich, Ben-Gvir) opposed |

## On-the-Ground Reality

The deal was announced *after* **Israeli strikes killed at least 9 people in southern Lebanon** and Hezbollah fired rockets into northern Israel. **Strikes continued Thursday** (2026-06-04) — at least one strike caused casualties in the south. Casualty numbers since the war began (Lebanese health ministry):

- **3,516 killed in Lebanon** (combatants + civilians combined)
- **1,000,000+ registered displaced**
- Israeli evacuation orders cover **>12.5% of Lebanon's territory**
- **26 Israeli soldiers + 4 Israeli civilians** killed

## Domestic Israeli Pushback

- **Itamar Ben-Gvir** (National Security Minister, far-right) called the deal a "serious mistake" that would allow Hezbollah to "grow stronger."
- **Bezalel Smotrich** (Finance Minister, far-right) also opposed — same day announced plans for **2,162 settler homes in the occupied West Bank**, signaling the right is responding to the ceasefire by accelerating settlement expansion rather than accepting a deal that constrains their maximalist agenda.
