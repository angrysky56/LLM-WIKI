# Israel Strikes Beirut Again — Ceasefire Collapses

**Date**: June 8, 2026  
**Sources**: BBC, New York Times, Al Jazeera  
**Significance**: Israel launched new airstrikes on Beirut suburbs on June 8, days after a US-brokered ceasefire was announced between Israel and Hezbollah. The strikes follow Hezbollah rocket fire into northern Israel and directly triggered Iran's missile retaliation (see [[iran-launches-missiles-at-israel-june-8-2026]]).

## What Happened

- Israeli warplanes struck targets in Beirut's southern suburbs, a Hezbollah stronghold
- The IDF stated the strikes were "in response to continued Hezbollah fire on Israeli territory"
- This is the second Israeli strike on Beirut within days — the first on June 7 also targeted the southern suburbs
- Hezbollah confirmed its fighters had launched rockets into Israel, accusing Israel of violating the ceasefire first

## Significance

The US-brokered ceasefire appears to have collapsed within 48 hours of its announcement. The sequence — Israel strikes Beirut → Hezbollah rocket fire → Israel strikes again → Iran launches missiles — represents the most dangerous escalation spiral in the region since April. The collapse creates a multi-front crisis for the US, which is simultaneously managing Iran nuclear talks and Ukraine peace efforts.
