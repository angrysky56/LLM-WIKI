## Kenya Ebola Quarantine Crisis: Protester Shot Dead at US Facility (June 11, 2026)

Anger is mounting in Kenya over a proposed US-run Ebola quarantine center, with one protester shot dead during demonstrations. The crisis highlights growing African resistance to US biomedical infrastructure on the continent.

### Key Details

- **Fatal protest**: One person was shot dead as hundreds protested the planned US Ebola quarantine facility in Kenya. Witnesses confirmed the shooting.
- **Context**: The US proposed the quarantine center in response to Ebola cases among American personnel/patients in the region. But many Kenyans view it as an imposition.
- **Political crisis**: NYT reports the situation has escalated into a full political crisis for Kenya's government, caught between US demands and public anger.
- **Broader resistance**: Multiple analyses frame the protests as part of "Africa's growing resistance to US facilities" — echoing patterns seen with US military bases across the continent.
- **Pre-existing tensions**: The protests tapped into pre-existing anti-US sentiment and concerns about sovereignty.
