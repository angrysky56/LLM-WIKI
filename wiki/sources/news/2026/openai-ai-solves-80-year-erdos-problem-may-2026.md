# OpenAI's AI Solves 80-Year-Old Mathematical Problem — Mathematicians Amazed

## What Happened

OpenAI's system solved a major open mathematical problem that had remained unsolved for approximately 80 years. Mathematicians who reviewed the proof expressed amazement at the result. This follows:

- **Google Co-Scientist** (May 19, 2026): Multi-agent AI partner to accelerate research, launched by Google DeepMind
- **Gemini for Science** (May 24, 2026): AI experiments and tools for scientific discovery, launched by Google
- **Google's Co-Scientist** (May 19): Also designed to accelerate scientific research via multi-agent approach

The pattern — multiple AI labs demonstrating concrete scientific discovery results simultaneously — marks a turning point for AI's credibility as a scientific tool.

## Why It Matters

1. **Proof of concept**: AI solving a genuinely hard, long-standing open problem demonstrates AI has moved beyond pattern matching to genuine mathematical reasoning
2. **Scientific credibility**: Multiple labs (OpenAI, Google) now demonstrating AI-assisted discovery — the "AI for science" wave is producing tangible results, not just announcements
3. **Mathematical community response**: Peer review will be critical — if the proof holds, it validates AI as a new kind of mathematical collaborator
4. **Competitive landscape**: OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic all positioning AI for scientific discovery — this becomes a key AI capability race
