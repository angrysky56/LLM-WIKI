# OpenAI Solves 80-Year-Old Erdős Problem — Mathematicians Amazed

## What Happened

OpenAI's AI system successfully solved an **80-year-old Erdős problem**, marking the first time an AI has proven a major open problem in pure mathematics. The breakthrough was reported May 21, 2026, by The Guardian and Scientific American, with mathematicians describing the achievement as remarkable.

## Why It Matters

- **80-year-old problem**: The Erdős problem had remained unsolved since the 1940s
- **Mathematicians "amazed"**: Peer review suggests the solution is novel and mathematically significant
- **Science breakthrough context**: This follows Google's Co-Scientist multi-agent AI (May 19) and Gemini for Science (May 24) — AI for scientific discovery accelerating rapidly
