## Peru Presidential Runoff: Leftist Sanchez Takes Slim Lead (June 11, 2026)

Peru's presidential runoff election remains too close to call, with leftist candidate Sanchez taking a slim lead over Fujimori as vote counting could stretch for weeks.

### Key Details

- **Sanchez leads**: Leftist candidate holds a slim lead in the vote count against Fujimori.
- **Extended count**: Peru election officials warn the count \"could take from two weeks to the constitutionally permitted deadline\" — potentially weeks of uncertainty.
- **Dead heat**: NYT reports a \"dead heat.\" Reuters says the race is \"too close to call\" and the presidency likely hinges on contested votes.
- **Trump endorsement**: NYT reports the Fujimori campaign is running a Trump-endorsed, far-right playbook that alarms Colombian and Peruvian women's rights groups.
- **Broader context**: Peru has been in political turmoil for years with multiple presidents in rapid succession. The outcome will determine the country's trajectory between leftist populism and conservative economics.
