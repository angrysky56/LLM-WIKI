## Philippines Earthquake: Death Toll Rises to 37 Amid Hundreds of Aftershocks

The death toll from the powerful earthquake that struck the southern Philippines has risen to at least 37, with hundreds of aftershocks continuing to jolt the region. Officials warned the toll could rise further as rescue operations continue in landslide-hit areas.

### Significance

- Major natural disaster in a geopolitically significant region
- Aftershock sequence continues, hampering rescue and recovery
- Infrastructure damage could affect regional supply chains

### Key Details

- Initial reports had death toll at 15-32 across different sources
- Landslides caused significant damage in mountainous areas
- Tsunami alerts were issued but later lifted
- Hundreds of aftershocks recorded, some strong enough to cause further damage
- International aid offers beginning to arrive
