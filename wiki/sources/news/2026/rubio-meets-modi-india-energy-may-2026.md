# Rubio Visits India — Energy Cooperation Amid Iran War Disruptions

## What Happened

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio met with Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi during a visit focused on energy cooperation. The visit aims to help India compensate for oil shortfalls caused by the US-Iran conflict and subsequent Hormuz disruptions. Rubio is offering US liquefied natural gas and oil to fill the gap left by reduced Iranian crude imports, which India had relied on heavily.

Simultaneously, data shows H-1B visa registrations have fallen by 38.5% for FY2027, reflecting both immigration policy tightening and economic uncertainty.

## Why It Matters

- **US energy diplomacy as geopolitical tool**: The Iran war has created a vacuums in India's energy supply that the US is now actively filling — a direct strategic play to deepen India's alignment away from Tehran
- **Venezuela-India oil routing**: As noted in previous cycles, Venezuela has become India's third-largest oil supplier; this trajectory is accelerating due to Hormuz disruptions and US-Iran conflict
- **India's strategic position**: Modi is maintaining diplomatic flexibility — engaging with US energy offers while not fully abandoning Iran relationships
- **H-1B as leading indicator**: Sharp drop in visa registrations signals US tech talent pipeline concerns; Indian IT sector likely to face renewed pressure
