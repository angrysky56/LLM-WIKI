# Russian Economic Forum Overshadowed by Drone Attacks on St Petersburg

**Date**: June 7, 2026  
**Sources**: BBC (Steve Rosenberg)  
**Significance**: Ukrainian drone attacks on St Petersburg overshadowed President Putin's flagship St Petersburg International Economic Forum (SPIEF), with the BBC's Russia editor reporting the event was visibly affected.

## What Happened

Ukrainian drone attacks struck St Petersburg during the same weekend as the St Petersburg International Economic Forum (SPIEF), Russia's premier economic event. BBC Russia editor Steve Rosenberg reported from the scene that the attacks "overshadowed" the forum, with the war reaching Russia's second city in a tangible way (BBC). The strikes represent a significant Ukrainian capability demonstration — hitting deep inside Russian territory near the symbolic and economic heart of Putin's Russia.

## Why It Matters

1. **War reaches Russian cities**: St Petersburg is Russia's second-largest city, the birthplace of Putin's political career, and the venue of his annual showcase economic event. Drone attacks here carry immense psychological and symbolic weight — similar to the impact of drone strikes on Moscow in 2023-2024.

2. **SPIEF as barometer**: The forum's flagging attendance and overshadowing by attacks signal declining investor confidence and the war's drag on the Russian economy. Western sanctions, capital flight, and military spending are taking a cumulative toll.

3. **Ukrainian strike depth**: Hitting St Petersburg (~700 km from the Ukrainian border) demonstrates Ukraine's growing drone range and strike capability. This shift changes Russia's domestic security calculus and could force redeployment of air defense systems away from the front lines.

4. **Co-occurrence with Chornobyl strike**: The same day saw a Russian drone strike on Chornobyl spent fuel storage. The parallel — Ukraine hitting St Petersburg while Russia hits Chornobyl — illustrates the expanding geographic scope of the conflict.
