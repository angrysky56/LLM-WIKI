## Russia Threatens Sanctions as Pashinyan's Armenia Pulls Further from Moscow

Russia threatened economic sanctions against Armenia as Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan's government accelerated its pivot away from Moscow following his re-election victory.

### Key Developments

- **Russia response**: Kremlin threatened sanctions as Pashinyan's Armenia pulls further from Moscow (The New Voice of Ukraine)
- **Putin-Pashinyan meeting**: To be decided only after final election results are confirmed (Armenpress)
- **EU reaction**: EU congratulated Pashinyan on his election win (Oragark)
- **Russia condemnation**: Russia condemned the Armenian election (Oragark)

### Strategic Context

The EU Institute for Security Studies noted the election was "a welcome win for Pashinyan, but a difficult road lies ahead." The Armenian government has been progressively distancing itself from the Russia-led Collective Security Treaty Organization (CSTO) and seeking closer ties with the EU and NATO.

### Significance

Armenia's pivot represents a strategic shift in the South Caucasus. Russia's threatened sanctions would mark a dramatic deterioration in Moscow's relations with a traditional ally. The outcome could reshape the regional balance of power, affecting the Nagorno-Karabakh conflict dynamics and the Iran-Armenia border corridor.
