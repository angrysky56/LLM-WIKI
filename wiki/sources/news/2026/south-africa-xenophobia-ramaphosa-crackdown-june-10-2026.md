## South Africa: Ramaphosa Vows Crackdown as Xenophobic Attacks Escalate

South Africa's President Ramaphosa vowed to crack down on groups behind a wave of xenophobic attacks targeting foreign nationals, as 300 Ghanaians departed the country amid escalating tensions.

### Key Developments

- **Mass departure**: 300 Ghanaians leave South Africa amid xenophobic tensions (DW)
- **Ramaphosa response**: President vows crackdown on groups behind attacks (US News & World Report)
- **Human rights concerns**: Human Rights Watch documents new waves of xenophobic attacks
- **Academic analysis**: The Conversation warns that anti-migrant campaigns "use the language of democracy" — a dangerous framing that legitimizes violence

### Regional Context

Nigeria-South Africa tensions are rising amid the attacks (Al Jazeera). The outbreak adds to a global pattern of anti-immigrant unrest, with simultaneous events in Belfast (UK) and anti-immigrant policy in the United States.
