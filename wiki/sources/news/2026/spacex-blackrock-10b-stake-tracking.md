## What "Confirmation" Would Look Like

A confirmed deal would be reported as:
- BlackRock formally commits $X billion at $Y valuation
- S-1 filing updated with BlackRock as anchor investor
- SEC filing lists BlackRock in investor section
- Press release from either SpaceX or BlackRock

## Key Context

1. **Quiet period**: SpaceX is in SEC quiet period pre-IPO — cannot comment publicly. BlackRock also typically stays silent on pre-IPO allocations until formal filing update.
2. **Range ($5B–$10B)**: Sources consistently cite "$5B–$10B" range, not a fixed number — this means the upper bound hasn't been agreed.
3. **Timing**: S-1 updates in final days before listing typically include anchor investor names. If BlackRock is in, expect disclosure in S-1/A amendments June 7–10.
4. **Institutional dynamics**: BlackRock participating as anchor investor would signal institutional legitimacy for a company that has never been public and has significant governance novelty (Musk ~78% voting control).

## Pre-June 12 Monitoring Plan

- **June 3–6**: Watch for S-1/A amendments listing anchor investors
- **June 7–10**: Watch for any pre-IPO media confirmation from BlackRock or SpaceX
