# SpaceX Files for IPO — Starship V3 Pre-IPO Test Success (May 2026)

## What Happened

**SpaceX officially filed for IPO in May 2026**, with the filing becoming public around May 20. The company seeks a valuation in the range of **$1.75 trillion to $2 trillion**. Simultaneously, **Starship V3** — the largest and most upgraded rocket in the program — completed a successful pre-IPO test flight on May 23.

**Test flight outcomes:**
- Starship V3 (largest rocket ever built) hit most test targets
- Booster recovery attempted — partial success
- Mock satellite deployment via "Pez dispenser" mechanism successful
- Starship splashdown achieved
- First Starship launch of 2026 — successful on second attempt (initial scrub May 21)

## Financial Details

- **Starship development spending**: $15 billion+ total (Reuters exclusive)
- **IPO target**: $75B initial offering
- **Stock split**: 5-for-1 approved by shareholders ahead of IPO
- **Valuation context**: $1.75T-$2T places SpaceX among world's most valuable private companies
- **AI space data centers**: SEC filing acknowledges unproven AI space data centers may not be commercially viable — risk factor disclosed in IPO docs
