# SpaceX IPO Pricing Confirmed: $75B at $135/Share (2026-06-03)

## What Happened

Bloomberg (citing Reuters) reports **SpaceX is targeting $75 billion in IPO proceeds at $135 per share**, more than a week before expected trading. The **fixed-price** structure is a notable departure from the typical IPO range-pricing playbook — SpaceX is signaling **strong enough demand** to commit to a single number well in advance.

The pricing formalizes what the carryover thread ([[spacex-ipo-blackrock-10b-stake-may-2026]]) anticipated: SpaceX IPO trading window opens **2026-06-12** (next week). BlackRock's reported **$10B stake** remains the largest anchor commitment on record.

## Why It Matters

- **Largest IPO of 2026 by a wide margin**: $75B exceeds Anthropic's reported $1T filing *valuation* — Anthropic is the public-market event, SpaceX is the public-market event in *proceeds raised*. Two trillion-dollar-plus AI/space events in one quarter.
- **Fixed-price signal**: When a company prices above the range early, it's either (a) extraordinarily oversubscribed or (b) confident in scarcity. Both are signals of market froth — or fundamental demand strength, depending on view.
- **AI/space public-market correlation**: Anthropic (AI) and SpaceX (launch + Starlink) are the two biggest 2026 listings. The Bloomberg piece pairs this with [[anthropic-confidentially-files-ipo-2026-june-2]] in the broader "is this an AI/space bubble" framing.
- **Figma cautionary tale**: Anthropic's carryover thread noted Figma's post-IPO correction as the warning sign. Whether SpaceX holds its opening price is a major test.
- **Starlink/Starship optionality**: The $75B raise gives SpaceX capital to accelerate Starship cadence and Starlink Gen2 — directly relevant to Blue Origin (carryover [[blue-origin-new-glenn-explosion-may-2026]] delay story, since SpaceX is the dominant commercial launch competitor).
