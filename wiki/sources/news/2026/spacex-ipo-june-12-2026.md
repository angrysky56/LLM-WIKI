# SpaceX IPO: June 12 Nasdaq Listing Targets $75B Offering

## What Happened

SpaceX officially filed for IPO around May 20, 2026 and is now targeting June 12 listing on Nasdaq. The company seeks to raise approximately $75 billion in what would be the largest IPO in history. The filing reveals:

- **Valuation**: $1.75T-$2T target
- **Offering size**: $75 billion
- **Stock split**: 5-for-1 approved
- **Starship V3 test**: Successful pre-IPO test May 23 — hit most targets
- **Musk control**: Filing reveals Musk maintains extraordinary control; ~42% voting power (updated: governance question unresolved pre-IPO — SpaceX declined to clarify whether ~78% pre-IPO voting control persists post-listing)
- **AI bet**: SEC filing includes risk factor on AI space data center viability — acknowledges unproven commercial viability
- **Financial losses**: Pre-IPO disclosures reveal significant ongoing losses
- **xAI rebuild**: Musk turning to loyalists to rebuild xAI ahead of IPO (reported May 19)

The IPO prospectus frames SpaceX's future around AI-powered space infrastructure, not just rockets.
