## Core Insight

Europe is experiencing a sustained STI epidemic that is getting worse, not better. Gonorrhoea cases increased 303% from 2015 to 2024, while syphilis more than doubled. The driving factors — widening gaps in testing and prevention — are structural, not superficial. A gonorrhoea vaccine exists (UK deployed it in 2025 after hitting 85,000 cases in 2023), but prevention gaps persist across the continent.
