# Tech Stock Plunge — AI Rally Cools Amid Middle East Escalation

**Date**: June 8, 2026  
**Sources**: Bloomberg, BBC, NYT  
**Significance**: Global tech stocks plunged on June 8 as the AI-driven rally cooled and renewed Middle East conflict (Iran-Israel missile exchange) spooked investors. The selloff extended across Asia, with Korean stocks tumbling as AI trades unwound, and Treasuries dropped as jobs data and Iran tensions fueled rate hike bets.

## What Happened

- Tech stocks plunged across Asia after a record rally, triggered by cooling AI momentum and the Iran-Israel escalation
- Korean stocks tumbled as AI-related trades unwound, threatening the bull run
- Treasuries dropped as strong US jobs data combined with Iran tensions to fuel rate hike expectations
- Oil gained as Middle East supply risk premium returned (Saudis lowered July prices but still at decades-high levels)
- Indonesia's market rout deepened with bonds, currency, and stocks all sliding
- Copper rebounded from a Friday slump on China-buying and US fund flows

## Significance

The simultaneous unwind of the AI trade and the Middle East risk premium creates a volatile environment. The AI rally had been the primary driver of equity gains in 2026; its cooling — combined with geopolitical risk, rate hike bets, and oil price pressure — represents a triple headwind for global markets. Key indicator to watch: whether the AI unwind is a correction or a structural rotation.
