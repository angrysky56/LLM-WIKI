# Trump Administration Proposes 25% Tariff on Brazil (2026-06-02)

## Significance

USTR announced a 25% tariff on Brazilian goods "to punish Brazil over trade practices" (Reuters). The framing makes clear this is a foreign-policy tool, not a trade-balancing mechanism. It is the first time the Trump tariff doctrine has been deployed against a major Latin American democracy and a BRICS member, signaling an escalation in hemispheric political pressure.

## Key facts

- 25% across-the-board tariff proposed (NYT, Reuters, UPI 2026-06-02)
- Framing: "unfair trade practices" (NYT), "punish Brazil" (Reuters)
- Comes amid Bolsonaro trial developments; Trump positioned as Bolsonaro defender
- Coordinated with Colombia right-wing push (Espriella first-round 2026-06-01)

## Why it matters

- **Tariff as foreign policy**: Continues the pattern of using tariffs for non-trade ends (cf. China rare-earth leverage, EU renegotiation).
- **Latin America right-wing bloc**: Combined with the Colombia runoff, the Trump administration is consolidating a hemispheric right-wing coalition. The Brazil tariff punishes the holdout.
- **BRICS response test**: Brazil has BRICS diplomatic cover to retaliate. Other BRICS members will watch closely.
- **Judicial check**: Recent US trade-court rulings have constrained Trump tariff powers — the Brazil action tests that boundary.
