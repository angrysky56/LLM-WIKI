# Trump Endorses Colombia's Right-Wing Espriella for Runoff (2026-06-03)

## What Happened

Donald Trump publicly endorsed **Abelardo De La Espriella**, the right-wing surprise first-round winner of the 2026-05-31 Colombian presidential election, ahead of the **June 2026 runoff** against center-left **Iván Cepeda**. Espriella responded by **vowing closer US ties** (France 24, Colombia Reports).

The endorsement came amid the same week that Trump proposed **25% tariffs on Brazil** (2026-06-02), signaling a coordinated right-wing Latin America consolidation push from the Trump White House.

## Why It Matters

- **First direct US presidential endorsement of a Latin American presidential candidate in modern history**: EL PAÍS described it as "total endorsement." This breaks a long-standing norm of US non-interference in democratic elections in the hemisphere.
- **Carries through carryover 2026-06-01 thread**: Espriella's first-round "surprise win" (Guardian) was the seed; today's endorsement is the US-side commitment. The [[trump-brazil-25-percent-tariff-june-2-2026]] from yesterday and today's Colombia endorsement are **two pillars of the same right-wing bloc consolidation strategy** in Latin America.
- **Espriella profile**: Self-described "Trump admirer" (Guardian 2026-06-01). His first-round lead was a shock given Cepeda's polling lead pre-election. The Trump endorsement now cements the relationship.
- **Cepeda (Petro-aligned) risk**: If Espriella wins, Colombia shifts from the Petro-progressive Petro era toward explicit US alignment. Cepeda is the Petro-endorsed successor candidate.
- **Colombian cartel gold context**: NYT 2026-06-02 ran "U.S. Was Asked to Blacklist Colombian Cartel Gold. It Was Also Buying It." — the US relationship with Colombia is **already contradictory** at the policy level.
