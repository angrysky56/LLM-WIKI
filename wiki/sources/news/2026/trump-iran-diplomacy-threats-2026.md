## Core Insight

Trump's Iran strategy is defined by **oscillation**, not intention. Analysts describe a pattern where every few hours the administration changes position — threatening strikes, then pausing for negotiations, then signaling escalation again. This unpredictability is self-defeating: it undermines Tehran's ability to make concessions in private when it can't trust whether the US actually wants a deal or war.

## Escalation Trap

Trump faces a structural dilemma:
- **Escalate** → continued economic knock-on effects, declining approval ratings
- **Accept deal** → must be seen as going beyond the 2015 JCPOA (which he withdrew from in 2018)
- **Stalemate** → Iran retains Hormuz leverage; escalation trap beckons

The pattern the article describes: applying more force in a stalemate "offers the slim promise that applying more force can alter the equation in Trump's favor" — but this is a trap.
