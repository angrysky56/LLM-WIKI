## Ukraine, UK, France and Germany Outline Five Conditions for Lasting Peace

President Zelensky, UK PM Starmer, French President Macron, and German Chancellor Merz jointly agreed on **five principles** for peace talks with Russia. The framework was announced following a summit of Ukraine's closest European allies.

### The Five Conditions (BBC, Kyiv Post)

1. **Full ceasefire** and withdrawal of Russian forces from occupied territories
2. **Security guarantees** for Ukraine's long-term sovereignty
3. **Accountability** for war crimes committed during the conflict
4. **Territorial integrity** and restoration of Ukraine's internationally recognized borders
5. **European security architecture** — a long-term framework addressing root causes

### Context

The framework comes amid increased Ukrainian military pressure on Russia (deep-strike Flamingo missile campaign, Moscow drone attacks) and fracturing ceasefires in the Middle East. A former Ukrainian foreign minister noted Putin will likely not negotiate with Europe directly, preferring US mediation (The New Voice of Ukraine).

The US has stepped back from its previous leadership role in the peace process, creating a diplomatic vacuum that European leaders are now attempting to fill.
