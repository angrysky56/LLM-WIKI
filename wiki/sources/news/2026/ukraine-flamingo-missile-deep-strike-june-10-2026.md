## Ukraine Flamingo Missiles Hit Military Plant Deep Inside Russia

Ukraine confirmed it used domestically-produced **"Flamingo" cruise missiles** to strike a military plant 900 kilometers inside Russian territory. President Zelensky confirmed the strikes targeted both a military plant and an oil refinery, marking a significant leap in Ukraine's domestic long-range strike capability.

### Key Details

- **Target**: A Russian drone and missile electronics factory — crippling production according to UNITED24 Media
- **Range**: 900 km inside Russian territory (Al Jazeera)
- **Weapon**: Domestically produced Flamingo cruise missile
- **Simultaneous**: Moscow targeted for 3rd consecutive day by drones (ABC News)

### Strategic Significance

The Flamingo missile represents a shift away from dependence on Western-supplied long-range weapons. Ukraine's ability to strike 900km into Russia with its own production reduces the constraints imposed by allies who limit the use of their weapons for strikes inside Russian territory. The NYT characterized the campaign as "really hurting the Russians."

This development comes as European allies (UK, France, Germany) jointly outline five conditions for peace — putting pressure on Ukraine's negotiating position even as its military capabilities grow.
