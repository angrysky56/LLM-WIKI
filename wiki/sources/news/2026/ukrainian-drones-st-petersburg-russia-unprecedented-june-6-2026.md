# Ukrainian drones target St Petersburg in attack Russia calls 'unprecedented'

Ukrainian drones struck targets in and around St Petersburg in an attack Russia described as 'unprecedented'. The strikes, reported on June 6, 2026, mark a significant escalation in Ukraine's ability to project force deep into Russian territory.

Key developments:
• Russia's defence ministry reported downing 'hundreds of Ukrainian drones' as the St Petersburg International Economic Forum concluded
• Vladimir Putin stated there is 'no point' meeting Zelensky over ending the Ukraine war
• The attack comes alongside continued Ukrainian drone operations targeting Russian border regions and Crimea

The operation demonstrates Ukraine's expanding strike range and Russia's vulnerability in its second-largest city, a symbolic and strategic target just months after earlier drone operations targeted St Petersburg during the SPIEF forum in early June.
