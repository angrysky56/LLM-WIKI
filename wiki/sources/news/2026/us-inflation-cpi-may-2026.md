## US Inflation Accelerates in May While Core CPI Softens

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that US consumer prices increased as expected in May 2026. Headline inflation accelerated to **3.4% YoY** — its fastest pace in three years — while the core CPI gauge (excluding food and energy) came in softer than expected at **3.1% YoY**.

### Market Reaction

- Bond traders kept bets on a Federal Reserve rate hike this year following the mixed CPI report (Bloomberg)
- Stocks pared losses after the tame inflation report: Markets Wrap (Bloomberg)
- Gold braced for the CPI print as rate hike fears mounted (KITCO)
- Bitcoin slipped holds $61,000 as rate cut expectations cooled (CoinDesk)

### Implications

The mixed data — headline rising but core softening — leaves the Fed in a cautious position. BNP Paribas previously projected three rate hikes starting December 2026 (from the carryover). The "re-acceleration" scenario challenges the market's prior expectations of rate cuts and directly impacts the AI IPO pipeline ($3.6T), as higher rates increase the cost of capital for growth companies.
