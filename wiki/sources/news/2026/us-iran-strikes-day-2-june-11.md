## US-Iran Military Escalation — Day 2 (June 11, 2026)

The US and Iran exchanged military strikes for the second consecutive day, marking a dramatic escalation in the conflict that had been approaching ceasefire negotiations as recently as last week.

### Key Developments

- **Iran closes Strait of Hormuz**: Iran officially shut the strait through which ~20% of global oil transits in peacetime. Al Jazeera reports the question is now moot — the strait was effectively closed by combat operations.
- **Iran targets US allies**: Iran struck American assets in Bahrain (damage to Manama), Kuwait, and Jordan. Video evidence shows damage to civilian infrastructure in Bahrain.
- **Trump threatens more strikes**: Trump vowed additional military action, with Bessent (Treasury) saying the US will "extract funds from Iran accounts if needed."
- **Oil markets whipsawed**: Bloomberg reports stocks rose cautiously as Iran risks whipsawed oil markets. Energy prices are the central concern.
- **Ceasefire talks reported**: Reuters reports that despite the attacks, ceasefire negotiations "intensify" — though Iranian sources say talks are ongoing alongside military operations.
- **Iran War Day 104**: The conflict reached its 104th day, making it one of the longest sustained US-Iran military engagements since the 1980s Tanker War.
