# US Jobs Data Triggers Full Fed Rate Hike Pricing

**Date**: June 5, 2026  
**Sources**: Bloomberg  
**Significance**: Macro regime shift — first full rate-hike pricing since the Fed paused; S&P record run in danger.

## What Happened

The US May employment report came in stronger than anticipated, signaling further labor market resiliency. Traders in the $31 trillion Treasuries market fully priced in a Federal Reserve interest-rate hike by the end of this year — the first time rate-hike expectations have been fully discounted since the Fed's extended pause (Bloomberg). The dollar advanced immediately.

Simultaneously, the S&P 500's record weekly win streak was in danger as a rotation out of artificial-intelligence stocks continued. European natural gas prices headed for a weekly advance as uncertainty over the US-Iran peace deal dominated sentiment.

## Why It Matters

1. **Rate hike U-turn**: Markets have been pricing cuts throughout 2026. A full hike-pricing represents a complete reversal of the dovish narrative. If the Fed follows through, it would be the first hike in the current cycle since the pause began.

2. **AI selloff intensifies**: The rotation out of AI stocks coincides with the rate-hike repricing — higher rates compress growth-stock valuations. This is the same AI rotation Bloomberg flagged June 4.

3. **Treasury market signal**: The $31T market is the deepest, most liquid signal of rate expectations. A full pricing means the vast majority of market participants now expect a hike.

4. **Dollar strength compounds**: Higher rates + stronger dollar = tighter financial conditions globally. Emerging markets (already strained by Iran war food crisis, per Al Jazeera June 5) face additional pressure.
