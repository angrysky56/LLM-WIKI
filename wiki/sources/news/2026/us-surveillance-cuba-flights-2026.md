# US Military Surveillance Aircraft Tracked Near Cuba

## What Happened

Tracking data shows multiple flights by US Navy reconnaissance jets and drones during the last week near Cuban airspace. The surveillance flights come as tensions between the US and China over Cuba escalate. The US has been pressing China over its relationship with Havana, and China has condemned US indictments of former Cuban leader Raúl Castro on murder charges.

This surveillance pattern represents a significant increase in US intelligence gathering near a strategic partner of China. China and Russia both condemned the US indictment of Raúl Castro, with Beijing characterizing it as another move to pressure the Cuban regime.
