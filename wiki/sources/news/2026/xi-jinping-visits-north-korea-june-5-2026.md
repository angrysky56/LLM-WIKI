# Xi Jinping to Meet Kim Jong Un in Rare North Korea Visit

**Date**: June 5, 2026  
**Sources**: BBC, New York Times, Al Jazeera  
**Significance**: First Xi-Kim summit since Kim's "exponential" nuclear arsenal announcement; Xi's first DPRK visit after meeting Trump and Putin.

## What Happened

China's Xi Jinping is set to visit North Korea for a rare summit with Kim Jong Un, taking place weeks after Xi met the leaders of the US and Russia — two countries that loom large over North Korea's foreign policy (BBC). The visit comes just one day after Kim unveiled a new uranium-enrichment facility and called for "exponential" nuclear arsenal expansion.

## Why It Matters

1. **Timing is everything**: The visit follows Kim's June 4 disclosure of a third uranium-enrichment site — the first use of "exponential" in public nuclear rhetoric. Analyst consensus (NK News' O'Carroll, per yesterday's coverage) saw the disclosure as timed specifically to foreclose denuclearization on the eve of Xi's arrival.

2. **Strategic positioning**: Xi meets Kim after meeting both Trump and Putin. This three-way diplomacy places China as the central broker between the US, Russia, and North Korea — all three nuclear-armed states in various degrees of tension with each other.

3. **Russia factor**: North Korea's deepening alliance with Russia has reduced Kim's dependence on China. The visit signals Xi's attempt to reassert influence over Pyongyang after Kim prioritized the Russia relationship (arms transfers, satellite tech, energy).

4. **South Korea dimension**: South Korea just held local elections (June 3) where the Democratic Party swept; the domestic nuclear-weapons debate is accelerating. Xi's visit directly affects that calculus.
