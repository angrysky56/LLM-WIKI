## Core Insight

A Canadian passenger aboard a cruise ship that had already experienced a hantavirus outbreak tested positive for the virus — highlighting that cruise ships, with their enclosed ventilation systems and high-density occupancy, create conditions where respiratory hantavirus (typically rodent-borne) can transmit person-to-person in ways rare in other settings.
