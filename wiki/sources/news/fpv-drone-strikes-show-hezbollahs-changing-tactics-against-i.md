## Core Insight

Hezbollah's released footage of FPV (first-person-view) drone strikes against Israeli targets shows a qualitative shift in the group's tactics — from imprecise rocket barrages to surgical, targeted strikes using commercial-grade drone technology adapted for warfare — making air defense much harder because the signature is smaller and the hit probability higher per munition.
