## Core Insight

The DR Congo Ebola outbreak is concerning not because of raw case numbers but because the **Bundibugyo strain** — rarer and less lethal than Zaire — is now spreading in urban areas and regions with porous borders, complicating contact-tracing and cross-border monitoring that previously relied on the better-characterized Zaire strain.
