## Core Insight

Boko Haram carried out a mass kidnapping of over 50 schoolchildren, including toddlers, in a pre-dawn raid on Mussa town in northeastern Nigeria — the latest in a decade-long pattern of mass abductions of children from schools and villages in Borno State. No group had immediately claimed responsibility, but the tactical signature matches Boko Haram's established playbook of targeting education infrastructure to sow fear and advance its insurgency.
