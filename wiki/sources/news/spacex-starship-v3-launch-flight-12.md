## Core Insight

SpaceX has scheduled Starship Version 3 — the largest rocket ever built, and the first to break the fully reusable megarocket paradigm at this scale — for its first launch as Flight 12. V3 introduces significant structural and thrust upgrades over V2, and on this flight the vehicle will for the first time attempt to visually inspect and document itself in flight — a novel self-examination capability that is a prerequisite for autonomous in-space servicing and rendezvous operations.
