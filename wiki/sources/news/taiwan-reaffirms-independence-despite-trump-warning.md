Taiwan publicly reaffirmed its de facto independence and sovereignty after Trump warned it not to pursue formal independence — illustrating the gap between Washington's strategic ambiguity on unification and the Tsai/Lai government's deliberate, public assertion of state identity that refuses to be muffled by great-power negotiation.
