## Core Insight

A drone strike was reported near the UAE's Barakah nuclear power plant — the Arab world's first operational civilian nuclear reactor — highlighting the vulnerability of nuclear infrastructure to cheap, proliferating unmanned aerial systems and raising stakes for regional security.
