# LLMSurgeon: Diagnosing Data Mixture of Large Language Models

**Paper**: LLMSurgeon: Diagnosing Data Mixture of Large Language Models  
**arXiv**: [2605.30348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30348)  
**Authors**: Yaxin Luo, Jiacheng Cui, Xiaohan Zhao, Xinyi Shang, Jiacheng Liu, Xinyue Bi, Zhaoyi Li, Zhiqiang Shen — VILA Lab, MBZUAI; UCL  
**Published**: 2026-05-28  
**Categories**: cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG

## Executive Summary

LLMSurgeon formalizes **Data Mixture Surgery (DMS)**: given only text generated by a target LLM, estimate the domain-level distribution of its pretraining corpus under a predefined taxonomy. The key innovation is framing DMS as an inverse problem under the label-shift assumption — estimating a calibrated soft confusion matrix and solving a constrained inverse problem to recover latent mixture proportions. This enables post-hoc auditing of model "digital DNA" without access to training data.

## Core Problem

LLM pretraining data composition ("digital DNA") is rarely disclosed. This blocks:
- Safety auditing for demographic biases
- Copyright infringement risk assessment
- Explaining performance disparities across domains

Existing tools (Membership Inference Attacks) can detect a specific document was in training but cannot describe the domain composition landscape.

## Technical Approach

### Data Mixture Surgery (DMS)

1. **Train domain classifier**: On predefined domain taxonomy (e.g., StackExchange, GitHub, Wikipedia, Papers)
2. **Sample from target LLM**: Generate text under domain-specific prompts
3. **Estimate calibrated confusion matrix**: Account for domain-specific language patterns
4. **Solve constrained inverse problem**: Recover latent mixture prior correcting systematic domain confusion

### LLMScan Evaluation Suite

Built from open-source LLMs with transparent pretraining mixtures (e.g., LLMA, Granite). Recipe-verifiable: ground truth known, can measure recovery accuracy.

## Key Results

- High-fidelity domain mixture recovery across LLMScan benchmarks
- Works with fixed protocols — no hyperparameter tuning per model
- Validated against models with known data mixture (Granite, LLMA)
- Label-shift assumption: domain proportions change, domain-specific language patterns remain

## Relevance to Agentic AI

As LLMs become central to social infrastructure, the inability to answer "What was this LLM trained on?" poses a fundamental challenge to trustworthy AI deployment. For agentic systems that make consequential decisions, knowing the data DNA is a precondition for proper oversight.
