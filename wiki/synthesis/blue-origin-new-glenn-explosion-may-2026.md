# Blue Origin New Glenn Explosion — May 29, 2026

## What Happened

Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket exploded during a test or launch attempt at Cape Canaveral on May 29, 2026. Initial reports confirmed the explosion; May 30 reporting from Reuters confirms the damage is substantial — the launch pad itself was damaged, and Blue Origin is now facing **months of delays**.

## Why It Matters

1. **NASA Moon mission timeline**: NASA's Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) depends on Blue Origin for cargo and payload deliveries. Months of delays directly impact the already-strained Artemis lunar return schedule.

2. **Competitive dynamics**: SpaceX's dominant position in the US commercial launch market is strengthened by Blue Origin's ongoing struggles. Blue Origin was positioned as a reliable secondary provider.

3. **National security space**: Blue Origin has been competing for national security launch contracts (NSSL). The explosion and delays may affect those competitions.

4. **Reputation**: New Glenn had been working toward a more operational cadence. This explosion is a significant setback.
