## Cross-Domain Analysis — June 11, 2026

### The Energy Shock Nexus

The dominant pattern of the day is the **energy shock cascade**: Iran closes the Strait of Hormuz → oil prices spike → ECB hikes rates for the first time in 3 years → bond markets price in a Fed hike → inflation narrative shifts from transitory to structural.

This is a genuine multi-domain cascade: **geopolitics (Iran war)** → **energy markets ($120+ oil)** → **monetary policy (ECB hike, Fed pivot)** → **equity markets (tech selloff, SpaceX rotation)**.

### The Overextension Pattern

Three stories in one day reflect a US that is militarily and diplomatically overextended:
1. **Iran war**: Second day of strikes, Hormuz closure, multi-country theater
2. **Kenya Ebola protests**: US biomedical facility triggers fatal protests
3. **Cuba/Hegseth**: Pentagon chief warning Cuba at Guantanamo Bay

Meanwhile the UK defense secretary resigns because the UK *isn't spending enough on defense*. The Western alliance is simultaneously trying to: fight Iran, support Ukraine, maintain NATO posture, run biomedical operations in Africa, and pressure Cuba — at a time of tight monetary policy and rising inflation.

### AI/Tech vs. Inflation Narrative

The AI selloff (carryover item) now maps onto a broader pattern: **retail rotation out of Big Tech into SpaceX IPO**. This isn't a bear market yet — it's sector rotation. But the ECB hike + PPI acceleration means the inflation narrative is winning for now. The question for the Fed meeting: does PPI + oil price = rate hike probability, or does the Iran war uncertainty = rate hold?

### Climate-Conflict Overlap

El Niño's official declaration overlays a climate crisis on top of the security crisis. Severe weather will affect:
- **Agricultural commodity prices** (adding to inflation pressure)
- **Peru** (extreme flooding/drought in an already unstable political transition)
- **Indonesia** (already lost 7% of rare orangutans to extreme rain)
- **Global bushfire risk** (Australia, California, Amazon)
