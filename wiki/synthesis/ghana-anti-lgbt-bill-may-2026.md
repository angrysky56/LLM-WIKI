# Ghana Anti-LGBT Bill — May 30, 2026

## What Happened

Ghana's parliament passed a bill criminalizing gay acts on May 30, 2026. The legislation awaits presidential signature to become law, which would activate criminal penalties including imprisonment.

## Why It Matters

1. **Regional trajectory**: Ghana has been considered one of Africa's more stable democracies and a regional leader on democratic governance. This legislative move aligns it with more restrictive African nations (Nigeria, Uganda) and puts it in tension with South Africa's constitutional gay rights protections.

2. **International relations**: Passage puts Ghana in potential conflict with Western diplomatic partners, aid frameworks, and international human rights organizations. The presidential signature decision is the pivotal next point.

3. **Broader trend**: The bill reflects a broader wave of anti-LGBT legislation across Africa — not a reversal of an existing open climate, but part of an accelerating restriction pattern.

## Key Facts
- Parliament passed: May 30, 2026
- Awaiting: Presidential signature
- Penalty: Imprisonment for gay acts
- International reaction: Not yet formalized
