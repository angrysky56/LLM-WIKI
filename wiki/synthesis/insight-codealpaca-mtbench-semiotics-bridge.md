## CodeAlpaca and MT-Bench Bridging LLM Evaluation and Human Language Theory

The community cluster linking CodeAlpaca, MT-Bench, and language modeling with semiotic theory from the "Language Models as Semiotic Machines" paper reveals an unexpected bridge between pragmatic LLM evaluation benchmarks and fundamental questions about how language models handle meaning. MT-Bench and CodeAlpaca serve as concrete testbeds for measuring capabilities that (as the semiotic analysis argues) may be fundamentally limited by the gap between statistical pattern matching and true semiotic understanding. This suggests that benchmark evaluations inadvertently test philosophical assumptions about meaning — a CodeAlpaca score is not just a performance metric but an implicit measurement of the model's relationship to signification and reference.

### Evidence

- CodeAlpaca benchmark linked to semiotic theory cluster
- MT-Bench evaluation connected to Language Models as Semiotic Machines analysis
- Bridge between pragmatic LLM evaluation and theoretical linguistics
- Capability measurement as implicit semiotic testing
- Statistical pattern matching vs. true understanding framing
