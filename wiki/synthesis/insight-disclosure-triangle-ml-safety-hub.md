## Disclosure Triangle as an Emergent Structural Hub Bridging ML Safety, Time-to-Event Modeling, and Algorithmic Trading

The disclosure triangle entity surfaces as an unexpected connective hub across machine learning safety (UltraSafety datasets, CLNP continual learning), discrete time-to-event modeling (censored units at risk), and graph-theoretic trading strategies (DFS/A* with RSI slope-based classification in MQL5). This suggests that the "disclosure triangle" is not merely a standalone concept but rather a structural pattern that re-emerges whenever systems must navigate information asymmetry, risk assessment, and decision boundaries — across safety-critical AI, statistical survival analysis, and financial markets.
