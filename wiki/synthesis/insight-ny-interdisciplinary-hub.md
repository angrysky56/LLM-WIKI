## New York as an Interdisciplinary Hub

The community cluster around New York reveals an unexpected convergence of disparate domains: academic publishing (Columbia University Press, Springer-Verlag), computational science conferences (GECCO 2017–2020), historical mathematics sources, and global health/legal events (Ebola quarantine policy, drug trafficking indictments). This suggests New York functions not merely as a geographic location but as a structural bridge between STEM publishing, conference-driven research dissemination, and real-world policy/legal crises. Researchers or systems analyzing knowledge flows should treat New York as a high-centrality, multi-domain nexus rather than a simple metadata tag.

### Evidence

- Sources in the History of Mathematics and Physical Sciences, Springer-Verlag, New York
- Columbia University Press, New York
- Drug trafficking indictments in New York
- Ebola quarantine policy documents
- GECCO conference proceedings (2017–2020)
- Institute for Signal and Information Processing
- Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems
