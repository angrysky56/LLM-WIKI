## Quantum Reconstruction and GPTs Form a Coherent Foundational Cluster

The Quantum Reconstruction Program and Generalized Probabilistic Theories (GPTs) are structurally bound together, not merely as adjacent topics but as interdependent pillars of the same research program. The evidence shows that foundational reviews and expository materials treat them as a single intellectual arc: GPTs provide the mathematical framework within which quantum theory's reconstruction is pursued. This implies that any analysis of the foundations of quantum mechanics that separates these strands misses the core methodological insight — that quantum theory emerges as a special case within a broader landscape of possible probabilistic theories. The reconstruction program is inseparable from the GPT framework that enables it.
