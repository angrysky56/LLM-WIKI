## Core Insight

The US sanctions on ICC Prosecutor Francesca Albanese (announced June 2025) are not an isolated diplomatic spat — they are the **retaliation layer** in a coordinated impunity-protection pattern that connects directly to Israeli military operations in southern Lebanon.

The same cluster (community_65) surfaces:
1. **Israeli ground operations in four Lebanese villages** (Yaroun, Maroun al-Ras, Aita al-Shaab, Blida — all in Tyre district)
2. **The Hannaouiyah paramedic attack** (15 killed, including Red Cross/defense workers)
3. **ICC arrest warrants for Netanyahu & Gallant** (Nov 2024, war crimes in Gaza)
4. **US sanctions on Albanese** (June 2025, framed as "protecting allies")

These are not separate news items. They are **causal links in a single accountability-evasion chain**.

1. **Accountability is operationally contingent** — not just legally contested. The more documented the operations (UN observers, Red Cross, paramedic bodies), the sharper the political retaliation against the court.
