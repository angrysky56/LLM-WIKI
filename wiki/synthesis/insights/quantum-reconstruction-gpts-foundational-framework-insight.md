## Core Synthesis

The Quantum Reconstruction Program and Generalized Probabilistic Theories (GPTs) form a tightly-coupled knowledge community, with supporting evidence showing they are introduced together as co-foundational frameworks. The Quantum Reconstruction Program seeks to derive quantum theory from operational principles (information-theoretic postulates rather than physical axioms), while GPTs provide the mathematical language for describing any probabilistic physical theory. Together, they represent a **meta-framework where quantum mechanics is not fundamental but emergent from operational constraints**.

## The Framework Chain

1. **Generalized Probabilistic Theories** define the space of possible physical theories
2. **The Quantum Reconstruction Program** shows that quantum theory is a special point in this space
3. **GPTs give the language**; reconstruction gives the derivation
4. **Together**: quantum mechanics is not discovered but reconstructed from information-theoretic principles

## Novel Connection

This framework positions large language models' probabilistic inference as sharing structural features with GPT representations of physical theories—suggesting that LLMs may implicitly learn operational principles analogous to those that constrain physical theory reconstruction.
