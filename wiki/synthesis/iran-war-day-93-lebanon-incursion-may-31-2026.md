# Iran War Day 93 — Deal Stalls, Israel Expands Lebanon Incursion

## Situation

**Trump Deal Status**: Trump told Fox News he is "in no hurry" for a deal with Iran, while US media reported he toughened the terms and requested amendments to the preliminary framework. Key remaining conditions unclear — but Trump insists on "no nuclear weapons" as the guarantee. An Iranian official accused Trump of stalling with "excessive demands."

**Israel Lebanon Expansion**: On the same day, Israeli ground forces crossed the Litani River and captured Beaufort Castle — the deepest incursion into Lebanon since 2000 (26 years). Israel issued new displacement orders south of the Zahrani River. Lebanon's PM accused Israel of a "scorched-earth policy."

**Iran Military Developments**: IRGC shot down a US drone in Iranian territorial waters. Iran unveiled a new 100-knot naval attack craft (Rajab 27) capable of launching cruise missiles. These developments raise the risk of incidents that could derail diplomacy.

## Why It Matters

1. **Parallel pressure tracks**: Trump is simultaneously squeezing Iran via sanctions/war pressure while negotiating — but Israel's expansion complicates Iran's incentive to accept any deal (why negotiate when Israel is taking territory?).

2. **US-Israel military integration**: Congress advancing a US-Israeli military integration plan could further entangle the US in Israel's Lebanon operations, reducing Trump's flexibility in Iran negotiations.

3. **Hormuz reopening condition**: The deal framework apparently includes Hormuz reopening — but the US naval blockade continues. Iranian state media reports $12bn in frozen assets as part of the proposed deal.

4. **Escalation risk window**: With IRGC shooting down US drones and new Iranian naval capabilities unveiled, there's a live risk of a military incident derailing the diplomatic track.
