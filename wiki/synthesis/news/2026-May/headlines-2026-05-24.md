### 1. Ebola Outbreak Escalation — SECOND Treatment Center Burned (Highest Priority)
**Outbreak deteriorating: two ETCs attacked, cases reach rebel-held areas**

This cycle brought qualitative escalation in the DRC/Uganda Ebola outbreak, now 8 days post-PHEIC declaration. New developments:

- **Second ETC set ablaze May 23** — 18 patients fled into active conflict zone. This is catastrophic: unmonitored patients return to communities, creating invisible transmission chains. The first ETC was burned around May 20.
- **Cases confirmed in rebel-held eastern DRC** — areas where armed non-state actors block health responders from access. These cases will not appear in official counts but are fueling silent transmission.
- **WHO Director-General held three high-level briefings in past week** (May 19, 20, 22) — calls for urgent Ebola action and pandemic preparedness.
- **American citizen infected and evacuated** (CNN, May 18) — first US national infection this outbreak.
- **US passengers rerouted** to Virginia, Texas, and Georgia for enhanced screening.
- **CFR published "A New Ebola Outbreak Spreads Through Conflict and a Weak U.S. Response"** (May 22) — explicitly framing US political commitment as inadequate compared to 2014-2016.

The burning of ETCs reflects community distrust — misinformation is a direct driver. This outbreak is effectively two simultaneous crises: a medical epidemic AND an access failure in conflict zones.
