# Anthropic-Vatican Alignment — AI Governance Fracture Deepens

## What Happened

Anthropic was the only major AI company formally invited to present alongside Pope Leo XIV at the May 26 rollout of "Magnifica humanitas." At the event, Anthropic's co-founder identified three ethical challenges of AI — a framing consistent with the Vatican's governance-focused message but in direct tension with the Trump administration's deregulatory AI posture. The Washington Post ran a separate story headlined "Anthropic aligns with Vatican over White House" on the same day.

Inside the Trump administration, VP Vance publicly called the encyclical "profound," while other officials expressed more skeptical or hostile reactions — CNBC reported "split" views.

## Why This Matters

**This is a realignment of AI company diplomacy.** Three distinct dimensions:

### Geopolitical: AI Companies Choosing Sides
Anthropic — a US company — is explicitly aligning with a multilateral governance actor (the Vatican) against the unilateral approach of its own government. No major AI company has made this kind of visible, institutional choice before. Google and Microsoft stayed quieter. Anthropic took the risk of direct Vatican positioning.

### Governance: The Vatican's New Role
"Vatican as global AI governance actor" has moved from theoretical to operational. The encyclical is not just moral philosophy — it's now a diplomatic instrument backed by 1.4B Catholics and diplomatic relations with 183 countries. The Anthropic alignment gives it a technical legitimacy partner.

### Internal US Government Division
Vance vs. other Trump officials on the encyclical signals the administration's AI policy is not unified. This matters for:
- Whether the White House responds to the Vatican's AI governance initiative
- Whether US diplomats will be instructed to counter or engage with Vatican framing at international forums
- Whether Vance is positioning himself as a more multilateral voice within the administration
