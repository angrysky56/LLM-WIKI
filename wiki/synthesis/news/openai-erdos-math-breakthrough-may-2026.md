# OpenAI Solves 80-Year-Old Erdős Geometry Conjecture — May 2026

## The Discovery

OpenAI's o1 model formally disproved a central conjecture in discrete geometry that had remained open since Paul Erdős posed it approximately 80 years ago. The proof was published by OpenAI on May 20, 2026. The solution required novel deep number theory — not merely computation, but genuine mathematical reasoning across combinatorics and number theory.

Terence Tao described the proof as containing "ingenious ideas." The mathematical community is still in the early stages of verification.

## Competitive Dimension

A separate but related development: Google's Gemini model solved a different 80-year-old math problem in the same period, completing it 9-to-1 faster than OpenAI in a comparative test run by The Rundown AI (May 25). This parallel achievement suggests:
