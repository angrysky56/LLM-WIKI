# Pope Leo XIV's "Magnifica humanitas" — AI Governance's New Actor (May 27, 2026)

## What Happened

Pope Leo XIV formally presented his first encyclical, **"Magnifica humanitas"** (Magnificent Humanity), on May 27, 2026. This is the first papal encyclical in history with AI as its primary subject. The document calls for binding international treaties for AI arms control and frames uncontrolled AI development as a "technologic threat to humanity." The title itself — magnificent humanity — signals the encyclical's anthropocentric frame: AI exists to serve human dignity, not replace it.

## The Unusual Scene: Anthropic Co-Presenting

The most geopolitically significant detail: **Anthropic co-presented at the Vatican rollout**. Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI were absent. This is the first time a major US AI company has visibly positioned itself alongside a non-US institution over its own government on AI policy.

The implications:
- Anthropic is betting that Vatican diplomatic machinery (1.4B constituents, 183-country network) is a more durable strategic partner than the current US administration for the long arc of AI governance
- The other three major AI companies made a different calculation — staying quiet, avoiding confrontation with the White House
- This creates a potential competitive differentiation for Anthropic in the Global South, in Catholic-majority countries, and in any future international AI governance framework

## The US Governance Vacuum

The context for this: Trump abandoned federal AI regulation on May 22. California moved to fill part of that vacuum with a proactive AI disruption prep order (60-day review ongoing). But neither the federal government nor US AI companies have engaged with the Vatican on AI governance.

The encyclical's framing — "disarm AI" — is now the default language for the world's largest non-US soft power institution. Without US engagement, that framing will shape the global debate.

## Vatican as AI Governance Actor

The encyclical isn't just moral argument — it's operational. The Vatican has:
- A 1.4 billion-person constituency (1/6 of humanity)
- Formal diplomatic relations with 183 countries
- A tradition of shaping international norms (nuclear deterrence, human rights, environmental ethics)

By issuing an encyclical on AI, the Vatican is doing what it did on nuclear weapons in the 1980s — inserting itself as a norm-setting actor before the major powers consolidate the framework. The encyclical creates a reference point for any future AI governance negotiation.

## International Reactions

- Vance called it "profound" — the most favorable response from any US official
- Other Trump officials were split — reflecting the broader internal division on AI policy
- No response yet from State Department on what Vatican AI diplomacy means for US foreign policy
